

   The final idea in Atomic Habits is about getting 1% better every day. This means you don’t have to make big changes fast. You just need to improve a little every day.

If you get 1% better each day for a year, you will be 37 times better by the end of the year. But if you get 1% worse, your results will drop quickly.

Big success doesn’t happen overnight. It comes from small steps done again and again. That’s the power of compound growth — small things grow into big results over time.

You may not see progress quickly. At first, it feels slow. But soon, your actions build up, and you’ll start to see change. This is called the plateau of latent potential — where success happens slowly, then suddenly.

So don’t give up if you don’t see quick results. Stay patient. Keep doing your small habits. Over time, they become stronger and more powerful.

Focus on being just a little better than yesterday. That’s how real success is built — not through big wins, but through small, smart steps repeated daily.
