After the dinosaur room, Lucy walks to the art gallery. There are many paintings on the walls.
Lucy sees a painting of a blue sky and green fields. She also sees a painting of a small village.
“Look, Mom! The colors are beautiful,” Lucy says.
Her mother smiles. “Yes, Lucy. Paintings can tell stories. You can imagine a whole world in one picture.”

Lucy reads a small description card next to one painting. It tells a story about a little boy and his dog.
Lucy likes the story. “I want to draw a picture like this,” she says.
She sits for a while and sketches the flowers and trees she sees in a painting.
“Art is fun!” Lucy says. She learns about colors, shapes, and imagination.
