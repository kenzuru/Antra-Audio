
  
 The next day, the boy stayed in the house. He searched for the golden ball again, but still couldn’t find it. He waited for night to come.

When the moon rose, he sat near the fire. The house was quiet for a long time, but suddenly he heard steps—loud and slow—coming again. This time, he did not hide.

Another giant came through the door. Bigger than the first one. It stared at the boy and smiled in a strange way. The boy stood with his sword ready.

The giant took one step forward, then another. But before he could reach the boy, the boy jumped forward and struck him hard. The giant screamed. His body broke in two.

The bottom half of the giant walked by itself over to the chimney and began climbing up. It was trying to escape.

“No, you don’t,” the boy said. He picked up the top half and threw it up the chimney after it. He watched as both parts disappeared.

Then he sat down and waited for the third night.
