

  Robert’s rich dad always told him that real learning does not stop after school. Many people believe that getting a good job and a high salary means success. But rich dad explained that money problems happen when people stop learning. Skills are more important than just knowledge from books.

Rich dad advised Robert to learn about sales, marketing, investing, and managing people. Even if you are not good at something, learning it can open new opportunities. For example, many people are afraid to sell, but sales skills help you in business, relationships, and even personal growth.

Rich dad said: “The more you learn, the more you can earn.” He encouraged Robert to take courses, read books, and find mentors. Learning from mistakes was also important. Every failure should teach you something valuable.

Poor dad, on the other hand, focused mainly on school degrees and certificates. He believed that the safest way to success was to work for others. But rich dad showed Robert that self-education, especially in money matters, gives freedom.

Robert learned that personal growth and financial success are connected. If you grow your mind, you can grow your money. Education is an investment that always pays you back.
