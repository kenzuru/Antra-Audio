
  
 On Monday, Lily takes the magic hat to school.
She puts it in her backpack and smiles.
“I won’t use it,” she tells herself.
“Just in case… maybe only a little.”

At school, her teacher, Mr. Jones, gives the class a math test.
Lily looks at the paper.
Some questions are hard.

She takes a deep breath.
Then, she slowly puts the hat on her head.

“Help me, hat,” she whispers.

Suddenly, the answers come to her mind.
One by one, she writes them down.
It feels easy—like magic!

Lily finishes the test first.
Mr. Jones looks surprised.

“Very fast, Lily,” he says.
“You must study a lot!”

Lily smiles and nods.
“Yes, I study every day,” she says.
She doesn’t tell him about the hat.

At lunch, Lily sits with her friend, Mia.
Mia looks sad.

“What’s wrong?” Lily asks.

“I forgot my lunch at home,” Mia says.

Lily looks around.
No one is watching.

She puts the hat on again and whispers,
“Give Mia a sandwich and juice.”

Poof!

A sandwich and a small juice box appear in Mia’s lunchbox.

Mia opens it and gasps.
“Wow! I thought it was empty!”

Lily smiles.
“Maybe someone is helping you.”

Mia laughs.
“Maybe I have a lunch fairy!”

Lily laughs too.
She touches the hat and feels happy.

But inside, she thinks,
“This hat is amazing… but I must be careful.”
