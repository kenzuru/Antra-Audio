
  
 Once, there was an old castle in the middle of a dark, big forest.
In this castle lived an old fairy. She could change her shape anytime she wanted.
During the day, she flew like an owl or moved like a cat. At night, she became an old woman again.

If a young man came near the castle—less than 100 steps away—he could not move. He was frozen like stone. The fairy would only free him if he promised never to come back.

But if a pretty girl came near the castle, the fairy turned her into a bird. Then she put the bird in a cage and hung it in the castle.
There were 700 cages, all with beautiful birds inside.
