At school, Mrs. Fluffy greets the rabbits. “Good morning! Today we learn about the weather.”
Ruby looks outside. “It is sunny!” she says.
Mrs. Fluffy shows pictures: sunny, rainy, cloudy, and windy.
“Repeat after me: sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy,” says Mrs. Fluffy.
Ruby repeats slowly, “Sunny… rainy… cloudy… windy.”
Then Mrs. Fluffy asks, “Ruby, what is the weather today?”
“It is sunny!” Ruby answers. Everyone claps.
Ruby notices the trees move in the light wind. The flowers shine in the sun.
During the lesson, Coco asks, “What do we wear when it rains?”
Ruby answers, “A raincoat and boots!”
The class laughs and practices saying the words together.
