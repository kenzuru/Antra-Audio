
  
  I sit in my usual seat in math class, and for the first time in my life… I’m scared of numbers.

Mr. Wilson writes some crazy-looking equation on the board and says,

“Let’s see if our math hero can solve this one.”

Everyone turns to me.

I laugh nervously. “Uh… maybe someone else wants a turn?”

But Mr. Wilson’s eyes sparkle. “Don’t be modest, Jake! We all want to see the genius at work.”

I look at the board. It's full of symbols that could be ancient alien language for all I know.

I stand up slowly, walk to the front, and stare at the board like it might bite me.

After 30 seconds of silence, I blurt,

“It’s... forty-two?”

The class bursts out laughing.

Mr. Wilson chuckles too. “Creative guess! That’s what I love — your boldness!”

I shuffle back to my seat, dying a little inside.

Emily still won’t talk to me.

After class, I try again. “Hey… can we talk?”

She shrugs. “About math camp or something?”

I sigh. “No, not math. About… us.”

She folds her arms. “Us?”

Great. Now I sound like we’re dating or something.

I try again. “Okay, look. That message Mr. Wilson got? The one about staring in class… it wasn’t about math.”

Her eyes narrow. “What?”

I lower my voice. “It was about you. I was trying to text you, not him.”

She stares at me. “Wait… you sent that to Mr. Wilson?”

I nod.

She blinks. “Oh my god.”

“Yeah. So when I said I stare in class, I meant I stare at… well, you. I just— I panicked after sending it. I didn’t know how to fix it.”

Her expression softens for half a second… but then hardens again.

“You lied for three days. You let the whole school think you’re obsessed with math.”

“I know,” I say. “I messed up. I just didn’t want you to think I was a total loser.”

Emily raises an eyebrow. “So you thought pretending to be in love with algebra was cooler?”

“…I didn’t really think it through.”

That night, I lie in bed, staring at the ceiling.

Somehow, my life has turned into a math joke with no punchline.

Mr. Wilson thinks I’m a prodigy.
Emily thinks I’m a weirdo.
And I’ve been nominated for Math Camp.

How did one message mess up my entire life?

There’s only one solution left.

I need to come clean… to everyone.

Even if it means humiliation.

Even if it means losing the gold star on my locker.

Even if it means… facing Math Camp.
