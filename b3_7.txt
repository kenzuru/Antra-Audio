

  Many people think goals are the key to success. But James Clear explains that systems are more important than goals. A goal is what you want, but a system is how you get there.

For example, a goal might be: “I want to lose 5 kg.” But a system is: “I walk 30 minutes every day, eat more vegetables, and drink water.” If you follow the system, the result (your goal) will come.

Goals give direction, but systems give progress. If you only focus on goals, you might feel sad after reaching them. But with good systems, you keep improving.

Also, goals are about the future. Systems are about now. You can’t control the future, but you can control your actions today.

So don’t only think about big dreams. Build daily actions that support your values. Small, repeated steps lead to big success.
