

  Sometimes bad things happen, like losing a job, having a fight with a friend, or making a mistake. These things may not be our fault. But the book teaches that we always control how we respond to problems.

Taking responsibility means we stop blaming others or luck for our problems. Instead, we accept our part in the situation and choose how to act next. For example, if someone loses a job, they can feel sad but also try to find a new job or learn new skills.

Taking responsibility is difficult because it means being honest and strong. But it gives us power to change our lives and feel free. When we blame others, we stay stuck and unhappy. Taking responsibility helps us grow and improve ourselves.

It also means accepting our feelings and actions. When we take responsibility for our feelings, we do not let other people control our emotions.
