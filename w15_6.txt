Lucy and her mother arrive at her aunt’s house. It is big and colorful, with balloons on the doors. Music plays in the garden.

Lucy opens the door. “Hello! Happy party!” she says.

Her aunt hugs her. “Lucy, welcome! Come and meet everyone.”

Lucy sees many relatives: her grandparents, uncles, aunts, and cousins. Some she knows well, others she meets for the first time.

Her little cousin Emma runs to Lucy. “Lucy! Look at my doll!” she says. Lucy smiles and shows the gift she brought. “This is for you, Emma!” Lucy says.
Emma opens the gift and claps her hands. “Thank you, Lucy! I love it!”

They play games in the garden. They run, jump, and laugh. Lucy feels happy to see her family.
