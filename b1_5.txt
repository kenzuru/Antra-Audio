

  Many people are afraid to fail or to be rejected by others. They avoid trying new things because they are scared to make mistakes or be laughed at. But the book explains that failure is normal and very important.

When we fail or are rejected, we learn what does not work. This helps us improve and find the right way. People who succeed do not avoid failure; they accept it and keep trying.

For example, if someone tries a new hobby or starts a business and it does not go well at first, they learn lessons and get better. Accepting failure helps us become stronger, more confident, and braver.

It is better to try and fail than never try at all. Failure is a way to learn and grow. When we stop fearing failure, we can take more chances and find happiness.
