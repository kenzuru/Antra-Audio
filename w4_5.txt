

 The night air was cold and heavy with the smell of pine and damp earth. Jake, Elena, and Derek sat around a small fire in the middle of the woods. The orange flames flickered, casting long shadows on their faces. They were exhausted but couldn’t rest. Lucas was missing, and the forest seemed to close in on them.

Elena studied the notebook under the weak light. “These symbols... I think they’re part of an old ritual or warning,” she said quietly. “Something to keep the Shadow Man away.”

Jake looked up. “Can you use it to stop him?”

Elena shook her head. “Not yet. I need more time to understand it. But the notebook talks about a place in the forest — a clearing where the Shadow Man appears every ten years.”

Derek rubbed his bruised arm. “We have to find that clearing before it’s too late.”

Suddenly, a crackling noise came from the bushes nearby. The three froze. Jake grabbed a stick, ready to defend. But it was only an old raccoon running away.

Still, the fear stayed. The woods didn’t feel safe anymore.

As they packed to leave, Jake’s phone buzzed.

A message from an unknown number:

“Leave the woods or lose more.”

Jake’s heart pounded.

“We’re not leaving Lucas,” Jake said firmly.

Elena nodded. “We have to be smart. The Shadow Man hunts fear.”

Derek looked into the dark trees. “We’ll stick together. No one goes alone.”

They started walking back when a cold wind blew out their small fire.

In the sudden darkness, Jake thought he saw a tall figure watching them from a distance — no face, just shadow.

They didn’t stop running until they reached Uncle Ben’s house.

The next morning, the town was calm, but Jake knew the danger was closer than ever.

At breakfast, Uncle Ben was quiet.

“Something wrong?” Jake asked.

Ben sighed. “Black Hollow has secrets older than the town itself. Your mother wanted you safe, but sometimes... safety is an illusion here.”

Jake wanted to ask more but stopped himself. The shadow was real, and it was coming.
