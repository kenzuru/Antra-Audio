

  This book tells a short story to help us understand change in life. It is told like a fun story, but it gives deep lessons.

There are four characters in a maze:

Sniff the mouse, who smells change early.

Scurry the mouse, who runs into action.

Hem the little person, who doesn’t like change.

Haw the little person, who learns to change.

The maze is like the world. The cheese is what we want — maybe a job, love, success, or money.

One day, all four find cheese at “Station C.” They go there every day and feel happy. But they don’t notice the cheese is slowly getting smaller.

Sniff and Scurry check the cheese daily. They are ready for change. But Hem and Haw think the cheese will last forever. They feel safe and lazy.

Then one day, the cheese is gone.

This is where the real story begins. Some are ready to move, and some are afraid. The book shows us how we can think and act when life changes.
