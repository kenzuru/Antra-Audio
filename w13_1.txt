It is Monday morning. Ruby wakes up early. The sun is shining, and the birds are singing outside. She stretches her legs and wiggles her nose.
She goes to the kitchen. Her mother has prepared a healthy breakfast: a carrot, some lettuce, and a small red apple.
“Good morning, Ruby! Are you ready for school?” asks her mother.
“Yes! I am ready!” Ruby says happily. She eats her breakfast quickly because she is excited to see her friends.
After breakfast, Ruby packs her bag. She puts in her notebook, a pencil, her red ribbon, and a small snack. She checks everything carefully.
“Don’t forget your lunch!” calls her father.
Ruby hops out the burrow. She feels happy and ready. “Today will be a fun day!” she thinks. She hops quickly to school, waving to the flowers and butterflies along the way.
