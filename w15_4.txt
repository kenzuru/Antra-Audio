Finally, Lucy goes to the history exhibit. She sees old clothes, coins, and small toys from many years ago.
Lucy asks her mother, “Who used these clothes?”
Her mother says, “People in the past. They dressed differently than we do today.”

Lucy looks at old toys. She sees a small wooden horse. “I want a toy like this!” she says.
Her mother laughs. “It is a very old toy, Lucy. But we can imagine playing with it.”

After a long visit, Lucy feels tired but happy. She waves goodbye to the museum.
On the way home, Lucy says, “I saw dinosaurs, beautiful paintings, and old toys. Today was amazing!”
Her mother smiles. “I am glad, Lucy. Museums are full of learning and fun.”
