After the lesson, the rabbits go to the playground.
Ruby sees a slide, swings, and a small climbing frame.
“Let’s play hop and run!” says Pip.
Ruby hops carefully over small stones. Coco runs beside her.
They take turns sliding down the slide. Ruby counts loudly as she slides: “One… two… three!”
Some rabbits play with a ball. Ruby joins them. She rolls the ball to Bella.
“Thank you!” says Bella.
Ruby laughs. She feels happy playing with her friends.
