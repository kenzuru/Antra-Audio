Tom walks slowly, following the map. He sees a small stream and a tree with a red mark. Under the tree, he finds a small locked box. Inside is a piece of paper that says: “Follow the bird.”

Suddenly, a bluebird flies above him. Tom follows the bird carefully through bushes and small stones. The bird stops near a small bridge. Under the bridge, there is a big stone with letters: “T-R-E-E-H-O-U-S-E.”
Tom writes the letters in his notebook. He looks up and sees a treehouse far away.
