If you found a potion that could make you live forever, would you drink it? This potion would keep you young, healthy, and safe from disease. At first, it sounds amazing. You could travel, try new hobbies, and do many things without worrying about time.

Some philosophers say living forever gives people freedom. You could reach goals and enjoy life without limits. John Martin Fischer says some things, like watching sunsets, never get boring, no matter how many times you see them.

But other philosophers warn about problems. Bernard Williams says life could become boring after doing the same things for hundreds or thousands of years. Changing your habits and your sense of yourself many times could also feel confusing.

Samuel Scheffler says that having limited time is what makes life meaningful. Because life is short, we have to choose what is important. Without limits, life could feel endless but less meaningful.

In the end, living forever could be both good and bad. It gives freedom and time, but it could also bring boredom and confusion. Choosing to drink the potion is not just about living longer—it’s about thinking what makes life truly valuable.
