

  Thinking about death is not easy, but it is an important idea in the book. Remembering that life is short helps us understand that our time is limited. This idea can change how we live every day.

When we know that we do not have unlimited time, we start to focus on what really matters. We spend more time with family and friends. We choose to work on our dreams and goals, instead of wasting time on small worries or unimportant things.

For example, if you remember that you might not live forever, you may want to tell the people you love how much they mean to you. You may also stop waiting to do things you enjoy, like traveling, learning a new skill, or starting a hobby.

Remembering death gives us courage to live honestly. It helps us make good choices and not waste our time on things that do not make us happy. This thought can help us be more thankful for life and live each day fully.
