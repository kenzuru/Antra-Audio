

 Many people want to be perfect. They want to always succeed, never make mistakes, and always be happy. But this is impossible. Trying to be perfect causes a lot of stress, anxiety, and sadness.

The book teaches that it is better to accept our mistakes and problems. When we accept ourselves as we are, with good parts and bad parts, we feel free and real.

For example, if you make a mistake at work, it is okay. You can learn from it and try again. Pretending to be perfect all the time can make us feel lonely or tired because we hide our true feelings.

Being honest about our problems helps us build better relationships. People like honesty and realness. When we stop pretending, we feel more connected to others.

Accepting imperfection means we are kinder to ourselves. It helps us stop being so hard on ourselves and enjoy life more with less stress.
