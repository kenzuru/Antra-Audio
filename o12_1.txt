
     
     A long time ago, a Queen sat sewing in winter. Snow was falling like feathers. She cut her finger, and three drops of red blood fell on the white snow. The Queen wished for a child as white as snow, as red as blood, and with hair as black as wood. Soon, she had a little girl called Snow White. But after the child was born, the Queen died.

A year later, the King married a new woman. She was beautiful, but she was very proud and cruel. She had a magic mirror. She asked it:

"Mirror, mirror, on the wall,
Who in this land is the most beautiful of all?"

The mirror always answered that the Queen was the most beautiful.

But Snow White grew up. When she was seven, she became more beautiful than the Queen. When the Queen asked the mirror again, it said: "Snow White is more beautiful now." The Queen was so angry and jealous that she could not sleep.

She called a Huntsman and told him: "Take Snow White into the forest and kill her. Bring me her heart." The Huntsman took the girl away. But when he was about to kill her, Snow White cried, "Please, let me live! I will run away and never come back." The Huntsman felt sorry for her because she was so beautiful. He let her go and killed a pig instead. He took the pig's heart to the Queen, and the Queen thought she had won.
