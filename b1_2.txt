

  Life is not perfect. Everyone has problems, pain, and difficult moments. Many people try to avoid problems or pretend everything is okay. But this book says that is not healthy. Instead, we should accept that problems are a normal part of life.

When we accept problems, we can face them honestly and find ways to solve them. This helps us grow stronger and learn new things. Happiness does not mean having no problems; it means choosing the right problems to work on.

For example, someone may have a hard job or lose money. Instead of being sad or angry all the time, they can accept the situation and work to improve it. Accepting reality helps us live with less stress and more courage.

If we do not accept problems, they can become bigger and cause more pain. Accepting problems is a way to stay calm and be ready for life.
