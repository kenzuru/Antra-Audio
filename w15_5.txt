Lucy wakes up early. Today is a special day. She is going to a family party at her aunt’s house.

Lucy jumps out of bed. She puts on her pink dress and her shiny shoes. She brushes her hair and smiles in the mirror. “I look nice!” she says.

In the kitchen, her mother is making breakfast: eggs, toast, and juice.
“Good morning, Lucy! Are you excited for the party?” asks her mother.
“Yes! I am very excited,” Lucy says. She eats quickly because she wants to leave.

Lucy packs a small gift in a bag. It is a toy for her little cousin. “I hope she likes it,” Lucy thinks.

After breakfast, Lucy and her mother get into the car. They drive to her aunt’s house. Lucy looks out the window. She sees flowers, birds, and big trees. “It is a sunny day,” she says happily.
