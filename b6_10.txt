

  In the last lesson, Robert explains that most people spend their whole lives working hard just to pay bills. They work for money and then spend it quickly. This keeps them in the “rat race.”

Rich dad taught him to think differently: work to create things that make money for you, even when you are not working. These are called “assets.” Examples are rental properties, businesses, books, or investments. They keep earning money while you sleep.

Poor dad thought the goal was to get a good job and save money in the bank. Rich dad believed that saving is not enough—you must use your money to buy assets that grow in value.

Robert also says it’s important to give back. Rich dad often donated to charities. He believed that sharing wealth can bring even more opportunities and blessings.

Finally, Robert reminds readers that becoming rich is not about luck. It’s about changing your mindset, learning about money, and taking action. He says anyone can improve their financial life if they are willing to keep learning, take smart risks, and focus on building assets.

The book ends with encouragement: start small, keep learning, and remember that financial freedom is possible if you think and act like the rich do.
