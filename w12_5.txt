The next day, Tom tells his friend Mia about the treasure. Mia is excited. “Can I see it?” she asks.
Tom shows the golden cup and the stones. They plan another adventure together next weekend.

Tom puts the treasure on his shelf and smiles. “There are many more adventures waiting,” he thinks.
Mia calls him. “Let’s explore the forest again tomorrow!”
Tom grabs his backpack. He feels ready. The forest is quiet now, but tomorrow it will be full of surprises.
