

  The cave was colder than Jake had imagined. As they stepped deeper inside, the flashlight beams cut through thick darkness that seemed to swallow the walls. Every sound — a drip of water, a distant whisper — echoed loudly, making Jake’s heart race.

Lucas led the way, his footsteps light but sure. “The lost kids are somewhere down here,” he whispered. “The Shadow Man traps them in a hidden chamber.”

Elena scanned the walls with her device. “The symbols here… they’re part of the binding spell. Whoever made this cave was trying to keep something locked inside.”

Derek tightened his grip on his bat, muscles tense. “Let’s find the others and get out.”

As they moved forward, the air grew heavier. It smelled of damp earth and old fear.

Suddenly, a faint cry echoed through the tunnel.

“That’s them!” Lucas said, quickening his pace.

They followed the sound to a narrow opening covered by twisted roots and stones.

Jake pushed the stones aside, revealing a dark chamber.

Inside, children — dirty, frightened — huddled together. Their eyes wide with fear.

“Help us,” one whispered.

Jake’s chest tightened. “We’re here. You’re safe now.”

But as they began to free the children, a cold wind blew through the cave.

The Shadow Man’s voice echoed.

“You cannot leave.”

Darkness surged from the shadows, swirling and twisting.

Jake held the dagger high, its blade glowing.

Elena began to chant the binding spell she had learned.

The children clung to each other, eyes wide.

The Shadow Man screamed — a sound of rage and pain.

Light flooded the chamber, pushing the darkness back.

For a moment, silence.

Then, the Shadow Man was gone.

Jake looked around. The children were safe.

But Jake knew this was only the beginning.

The shadow would return.
