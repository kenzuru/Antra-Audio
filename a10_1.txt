
    Zhangjiagang is a city in China that has found a smart new way to handle waste. In the past, people threw trash into the ground, which could make the city dirty and smell bad. Now, the city uses a special facility where trash is burned safely and carefully.

The facility is very clean, and it does not smell. A computer controls the whole process to make sure it is safe and efficient. People put food waste and regular household trash together into the system. When the trash burns, it produces electricity and heat. This energy is very useful. It can power about 60,000 homes and also warm small shops nearby.

The system does more than just create energy. After burning, some of the leftover materials are recycled and used to make things for building, like bricks or construction materials. Even dangerous waste, like chemicals or sharp objects, is safely stored so it does not hurt people or the environment.

This new waste system helps the people of Zhangjiagang in many ways. It keeps the city clean, reduces bad smells, and turns trash into something useful. Families and shops benefit from the energy, and the environment stays safer.

Thanks to this smart system, Zhangjiagang is becoming a model city for other places in China and around the world. It shows that with the right technology and planning, waste can be managed in a way that is good for people and the planet.
