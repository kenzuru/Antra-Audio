After lunch, the rabbits go outside to the school garden.
Mrs. Fluffy says, “Today we will count the flowers and learn colors too!”
Ruby looks around. She sees five red flowers, three blue flowers, and two yellow flowers.
“One, two, three, four, five!” counts Ruby, touching each flower carefully.
“Now the blue flowers. One, two, three!”
“And the yellow flowers. One, two!”
Ruby notices the shapes of the flowers too. Some are round, some are pointy.
Coco points to a flower. “Ruby, what color is this?”
“It is red!” Ruby answers.
They continue to count and say the colors. Ruby practices saying the words loudly and clearly.
The garden is quiet and sunny. Birds sing in the trees. Ruby feels happy to learn and play at the same time.
