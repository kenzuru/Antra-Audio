One morning, the Little Prince woke up early. He looked at his rose. She looked sad. “What is wrong?” he asked.

The rose said, “I feel small and lonely. I want someone to talk to.”

The Little Prince thought. “I can make the castle more fun,” he said.

He cleaned the rooms, watered the flowers, and made small paths in the garden. He also made a small playground for the rabbits. The birds liked it too.

Then he went to the tower of the castle. From there, he could see the stars and the mountains. He counted them: “One, two, three… many stars!”

The Little Prince learned that his castle is not only for him. It is for his rose, his plants, and the animals. When he cared for them, everyone was happy.

At sunset, he sat on the balcony. He watched the sky change colors: orange, pink, purple, and dark blue. The Little Prince smiled. He loved his castle, his rose, and his planet.

“I will take care of you every day,” he said. “This is my home, and it is special.”
