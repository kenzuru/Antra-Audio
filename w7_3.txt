
  
 The next day, Lily wakes up early.
She jumps out of bed and puts on the magic hat.
“Let’s see what you can do today,” she says with a smile.

She walks to the kitchen.
Her mom is there, cooking breakfast.
“Good morning,” Mom says.
“Good morning!” Lily says, sitting at the table.

There are two cookies on a small plate.
Lily looks at them.
She wants more.

She looks down at the hat on her head.
She whispers, “More cookies, please.”

Suddenly—pop!
Two more cookies appear on the plate.

Lily’s eyes go wide.
She looks at her mom.

“Did you make more cookies?” Lily asks.

“No,” Mom says.
She looks confused.
“I only made two.”

Lily says nothing.
She just smiles and eats one cookie.

Later, Mom goes to the living room.
Lily stays in the kitchen.

She puts the hat on again.
“I want a glass of milk,” she whispers.

A glass of milk slowly appears on the table.

Lily gasps.
“This hat is really magic!” she says.

She drinks the milk and laughs.
“I can make food with my hat!”

Lily runs to her room.
“I need to write this down,” she says.
“I must learn all the hat’s powers!”
