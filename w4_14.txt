

 Elena pointed to the well’s stone wall. “Look — there’s a loose brick.”

Jake pressed it, and a hidden latch clicked.

The well’s stone wall slid open, revealing a dark tunnel beneath.

The air smelled damp and old.

“Do we go down?” Derek asked, gripping his bat.

“We have to,” Jake said. “This is our chance.”

One by one, they climbed down the narrow stairs into the darkness.

Elena’s flashlight showed cold stone walls covered in strange symbols, glowing faintly.

The tunnel curved and twisted, leading deeper underground.

After a few minutes, they reached a large chamber.

In the center was an ancient altar, covered in dust and cobwebs.

On the altar lay the silver dagger — the same dagger from the cabin.

Jake stepped forward and picked it up.

The air grew colder.

Suddenly, a voice whispered from the shadows.

“You cannot stop me.”

The Shadow Man’s dark figure appeared at the tunnel’s entrance.

Elena’s hands shook as she read the notebook aloud — the binding spell.

Derek raised his bat, ready to fight.

Jake held the dagger tightly.

The Shadow Man moved closer, the darkness thick and alive.

Elena’s voice grew louder, stronger.

The symbols on the walls began to glow.

A bright light shone from the altar.

The Shadow Man screamed — a terrible, hollow sound.

Suddenly, the light exploded, filling the chamber.

When it faded, the Shadow Man was gone.

Jake, Elena, and Derek stood breathing heavily, unsure if the nightmare was truly over.
