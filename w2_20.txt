

 One year later, the bookstore was busier than ever. Maya had published her first small book of stories—Whispers of the Heart. It sat near the register with a handwritten sign: By Our Very Own Maya Winters.

Elias walked in carrying two coffees.

“For the author,” he said proudly.

Maya laughed. “Stop calling me that!”

“But it’s true,” he said, kissing her forehead.

Life hadn’t been perfect. There were arguments, bad days, doubts. But there was also laughter, growth, and deep love.

They had moved in together—above the bookstore, where it all began.

Lina had opened a flower stall nearby. Nico had left for the city, chasing his photography dream, but he still wrote to Maya now and then.

And as the sun began to set outside the shop windows, Elias pulled a small velvet box from his pocket.

Maya blinked. “What’s that?”

He opened it. A simple silver ring, delicate and beautiful.

“I don’t need a big moment or a crowd,” he said. “I just need you. Will you marry me?”

Her heart stopped for a second.

Then she smiled, tears in her eyes.

“Yes.”

They hugged, surrounded by books, dreams, and everything they had built together.

Love hadn’t come fast or easy—but it had come honestly.

And it had stayed.

The End.
