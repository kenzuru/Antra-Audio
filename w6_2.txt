
  
 Sunday morning hits me like a brick.

I wake up to the memory of last night — the confession text, the wrong recipient, the disaster. I grab my phone and scroll through the messages again just to be sure it wasn’t a dream.

Nope. Still there.

“I appreciate your honesty and passion. It's rare to see a student express such love for mathematics. Keep up the enthusiasm.” — Mr. Wilson

I groan and flop back into bed. My face burns just thinking about it.

I was supposed to confess to Emily. But now, Mr. Wilson thinks I’m some math-obsessed nerd who writes love letters to algebra.

And worse? I didn’t text Emily after that. I couldn’t. My courage had been used up and thrown in the trash with one stupid tap.

It was a bad night. I’d try again another day. Just… not now.

Monday morning, things get even weirder.

Mr. Wilson beams at me when I walk into math class.

Like, actually beams. I’ve never seen this man smile so hard, not even when he solved a quadratic equation in under five seconds last week.

“Ah, Jake!” he says, loud enough for everyone to hear. “The inspiration of room 204! Why don’t you come solve this equation on the board?”

The whole class turns to look at me. Emily too.

I hesitate. “Uhhh…”

“Come on now,” Mr. Wilson adds, winking. “Someone with your passion for math shouldn’t be shy.”

I nearly choke.

I drag myself to the board and scribble something that vaguely resembles an answer. Mr. Wilson claps like I just split the atom.

“Brilliant! You see, everyone? Jake is proof that passion leads to mastery!”

I slink back to my seat, feeling like a fraud.

Emily leans over a minute later. “Since when do you love math?”

Her voice is quiet, confused. A little… hurt?

I want to tell her everything. That the message was meant for her. That I spent all Saturday night thinking about her. That Mr. Wilson thinks I'm in love with numbers, not her.

But I just shrug and mutter, “I don’t know. I guess I’ve changed.”

She pulls away. Her smile disappears.

And now she thinks I’m ignoring her.

Great. Now I’ve got:

One confused crush

One overly proud math teacher

And zero idea how to fix any of it

And somehow, this week is just getting started.
