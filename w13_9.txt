While playing, Ruby sees little Max struggling to climb the small hill.
“Oh no! Max, can I help?” asks Ruby.
“Yes, please!” says Max. Ruby carefully hops beside him and pushes him gently.
“Thank you, Ruby!” Max says, smiling.
Later, Bella drops her small toy. Ruby picks it up and gives it back.
“You are very kind, Ruby,” Bella says.
Ruby learns that helping friends makes everyone happy.
She sits under a tree and eats her snack. The sun warms her fur.
