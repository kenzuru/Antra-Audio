At school, Ruby meets her teacher, Mrs. Fluffy, a kind grey rabbit.
“Good morning, everyone!” says Mrs. Fluffy. “Today we will learn about animals.”
Ruby looks at the big colorful pictures on the classroom wall. She sees a duck, a cat, a dog, and a bird.
“Repeat after me: duck, cat, dog, bird,” says Mrs. Fluffy.
Ruby repeats slowly, “Duck… cat… dog… bird.” She smiles. “I like the bird. It is small and yellow!”
Mrs. Fluffy asks, “Ruby, which animal is white like you?”
Ruby points to the cat. “This cat is white!” she says.
The other rabbits clap. Ruby feels proud. She learns the names of animals and notices their colors and sizes.
During break, Ruby talks with her friends, Coco and Pip.
“Do you like cats or dogs more?” asks Coco.
“I like cats,” Ruby says. “But dogs are fun too!”
They all laugh and play hop and run games in the classroom garden.
