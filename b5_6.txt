

 The last part is about how to live with less stress and more freedom. It teaches: “I expect nothing and accept everything.” This means you stop hoping that life will always go your way.

Many people get upset because things don’t happen as they want. When you expect something and it doesn’t happen, you feel sad or angry. But life is full of surprises and changes.

By expecting nothing, you don’t feel disappointed. By accepting everything, you learn to handle problems calmly. This does not mean giving up or stopping your goals. It means you stay flexible and strong, no matter what happens.

When you accept life as it is, you feel less stress. You can think clearly and make better decisions. You learn to enjoy the good moments and manage the bad ones.

This attitude helps you move forward. It helps you grow and be peaceful inside. So, say: “I expect nothing. I accept everything.” This way, you are free to live fully and happily.
