Jake was surprised. “Why?”

He paused. “My older brother disappeared when I was ten. I never told anyone because it was… hard to believe.”

Jake’s eyes widened. “You think the Shadow Man took him?”

Derek nodded. “I do. And that’s why I’m fighting back. I don’t want anyone else to lose someone.”
