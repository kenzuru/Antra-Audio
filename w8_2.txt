

 There was a beautiful girl named Jorinda. She was the prettiest girl anyone had ever seen.
A young shepherd named Jorindel loved her very much. They wanted to get married soon.

One day, they went for a walk in the forest to be alone. Jorindel said, “We must be careful not to go near the fairy’s castle.”

It was a beautiful evening. The sun was setting. Sunlight shone through the trees. The birds sang softly.

Jorinda sat and watched the sun. Jorindel sat next to her. Both felt sad but didn’t know why. It seemed they would be separated forever.

They walked far and did not know the way back home.
