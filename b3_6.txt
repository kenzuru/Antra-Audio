

  One of the best ways to keep a habit is to track it. When you track a habit, you can see your progress. James Clear suggests using a calendar, notebook, or app to mark every time you do the habit.

This is called "Don’t break the chain." If you do your habit every day, you make a chain of successes. Your goal is simple: don’t break the chain.

For example, if you study English today, mark it. Then tomorrow, do it again. After a few days, you’ll have a line of checkmarks. That feels good and gives you motivation to continue.

Even if you miss one day, don’t worry. The most important rule is: “Never miss twice.” One miss is normal. Two in a row is a new habit — the wrong one.

Tracking habits gives you a small reward, and it shows that you are serious. It also helps you see where you’re doing well or where you need to improve.

Building a habit is not about being perfect. It’s about being consistent. Keep going, even if it’s just a little bit each day.
