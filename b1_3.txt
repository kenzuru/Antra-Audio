

  Our values are the ideas we believe are important in life. They help us decide what is good or bad and what we should do. Good values help us live better and grow as people. Bad values can cause pain and confusion.

Good values include honesty, responsibility, kindness, and courage. These values help us face problems and build good relationships. Bad values may be wanting only pleasure, avoiding problems, or always needing other people’s approval. These can cause unhappiness because they do not help us face real life.

The book asks us to think about our own values and choose the ones that help us live a good and happy life. When we choose good values, we understand better what problems are important and which ones to ignore. This helps us live with purpose and happiness.

For example, if you value learning, you will work hard even when it is difficult. If you value honesty, you will tell the truth even when it is hard.
