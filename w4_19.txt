

  The days after the cave were tense. Jake felt the weight of the dark mark on his arm. It pulsed with cold fire, reminding him that the Shadow Man’s curse was still alive inside him.

Elena worked nonstop, trying to decode the notebook and find the ritual to break the curse. Her eyes were red from sleepless nights, but she refused to give up.

Derek trained harder, preparing for the fight ahead. “If the Shadow Man comes for us again, we’ll be ready,” he said.

Lucas helped around town, trying to reconnect with his family and the other lost kids now safe.

One night, Jake woke to a strange sound — whispers drifting through the window. He got up and looked outside.

In the moonlight, he saw dark figures moving silently between the trees.

The shadows were gathering.

Jake’s heart raced.

He knew the Shadow Man was calling for reinforcements — creatures born from fear and darkness.

He ran to Elena’s house and told her what he saw.

Elena’s face was grim. “We have little time.”

They gathered Derek and Lucas.

“We need to protect the town,” Jake said.

They set traps around the forest edge and prepared weapons.

The night grew colder.

The shadows moved closer.

A low growl echoed.

The final battle was coming.
