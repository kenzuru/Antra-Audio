It is the year 1972. People travel to the moon on the Apollo 17 mission. Astronauts explore the surface and bring back rocks to Earth. Scientists study these rocks carefully. Among them, there are tiny pieces called zircon. By looking at zircon, scientists learn new things about the moon.

The research shows that the moon is much older than people thought. It is over 4 billion years old. A long time ago, a very large object hit Earth. This collision caused pieces of Earth to break off, which eventually formed the moon. Scientists use special methods to measure the age of the rocks and understand when the moon was created.

Knowing the age of the moon is very important. It helps scientists learn more about Earth and how our planet developed. The moon also plays an important role for life on Earth. It helps keep the Earth stable and affects the movement of oceans and tides.

People are fascinated by the moon. Its beauty and importance make humans want to explore space and learn more about our universe. Studying the moon helps us understand not only the past but also how to protect our planet for the future.
