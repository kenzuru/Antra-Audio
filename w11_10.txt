

 Lena walked down the dark path. The trees whispered softly around her, but the terrible voices were gone. The fog lifted, and the moon shone bright.

She felt tired, but she kept moving. Her feet were heavy, but her heart was full of hope.

After what felt like hours, she saw lights in the distance—a small village.

She ran toward it.

When she reached the village, she saw people. They looked friendly and kind. She wanted to tell them everything. But when she opened her mouth, no words came out.

She tried again, but her voice was silent.

She was alone.

Lena looked back at the forest.

It looked peaceful, but she knew the truth.

The Whispering Forest was not just trees and fog.

It was alive.

It was a prison.

A place that kept the scared.

A place that took souls.

She sat down on a bench in the village square and opened the notebook again.

The last page had a new message written in fresh ink:

“Only the brave can leave. The rest stay forever. Thank you, Lena, for breaking the chain.”

Lena smiled a little. She was free.

But she knew the forest still waited for others.

And she knew she could never forget.

The End.
