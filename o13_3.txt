
      
    The witch did not see or know about the Prince's visits, because he only came in the evening. She found out only because Rapunzel made one simple mistake.

Rapunzel said to the witch, "Tell me, Dame Gothel, why do you feel so much heavier when I pull you up than the young Prince? He gets up here so quickly."

The witch was shocked! She screamed loudly, "Ah! You foolish child! What a terrible thing to say! I thought I had hidden you from the world, but you have tricked me!"

In her great anger, she grabbed Rapunzel's beautiful hair. She wrapped it around her hand, picked up scissors, and snip, snap, the long golden braids were cut off! They fell to the floor. She was so cruel that she took poor Rapunzel far away to a hot, empty desert where she had to live alone and be unhappy.

On the same day she sent Rapunzel away, the witch tied the cut hair to the window hook. When the Prince came in the evening and called the magic words, the witch let the hair down. The King's son climbed up quickly, but he found the wicked witch instead of Rapunzel. The witch looked at him with a cruel face and laughed at him. "Aha!" she cried. "You came for your love! But the beautiful bird is gone from the nest. Rapunzel is lost to you forever!"

The Prince was so shocked and full of pain that he jumped from the tower. He did not die, but he landed in thorns that hurt his eyes, and he became blind. He lived like this for many years. He walked sadly and blindly through the forest, eating only berries and roots. He did nothing but cry for his lost wife.

Finally, after many years of pain, he came to the empty desert where Rapunzel lived with her two twin children, a boy and a girl. He heard a voice, and it sounded very familiar. He went closer. Rapunzel knew him and threw her arms around his neck, crying. Two of her tears fell onto his blind eyes. Right away, he could see again, just like before! He took Rapunzel and the children to his kingdom. Everyone was happy to see them. They lived happily and peacefully for many years.
