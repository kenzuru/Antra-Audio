
  
  It is the last day before summer holiday.
Lily feels happy and a little sad.
She has learned many things with the magic hat.

She puts the hat on her head one last time.
“Thank you for helping me,” she says softly.
“You made my days special.”

Lily thinks about all the people she helped.
Her friends, the old man, the little boy…
She feels proud.

But now, she knows the hat is very powerful.
It is not a toy.

She walks to the park.
The wind is soft and warm.

Lily takes off the hat and puts it on a tree branch.
“This is your home now,” she says.
“Please help others, but be kind and gentle.”

She waves goodbye to the hat.
Then she walks home without it.

Lily smiles.
She knows the magic is inside her —
kindness, love, and care.

And this is the greatest magic of all.
