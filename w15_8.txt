Evening comes. The sun goes down. Lucy feels tired but happy. She hugs her cousins and her aunt.

“Thank you for the party,” Lucy says. “I had a very good time.”
“You are welcome, Lucy,” says her aunt. “Come again soon!”

Lucy and her mother drive home. She looks out of the window. She remembers the games, the cake, and the laughs. “I love my family,” she thinks.

At home, Lucy takes off her shoes and her pink dress. She eats a little snack and drinks water. She talks to her mother about the party. “Mom, I had so much fun today!”
