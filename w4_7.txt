
 
 The next morning, Jake met Elena and Derek at the edge of town. They had agreed to start looking for the old cabin Ben mentioned. The forest behind Black Hollow looked darker than ever. Shadows stretched between the trees like fingers reaching for them.

Elena carried her backpack full of tech gadgets and notebooks. Derek was ready with his baseball bat, and Jake felt the weight of the rusty key in his pocket.

“Are you sure this is safe?” Jake asked.

Elena shrugged. “Nothing in this town feels safe anymore.”

Derek cracked his knuckles. “We stick together. No mistakes.”

As they entered the forest, the air grew colder. Birds were silent, and the usual sounds of animals were gone. Only the crunch of their footsteps broke the quiet.

After walking for nearly an hour, Elena stopped. “Look,” she whispered, pointing to strange symbols carved into the tree trunks. The same symbols from the notebook.

Jake traced his fingers over one of the marks. “It’s like a trail.”

They followed the markings deeper into the forest. The trees grew thicker, blocking the sunlight. Every shadow seemed to move.

Suddenly, Elena’s phone beeped. She showed them the screen — a map with a blinking red dot moving ahead.

“We’re close,” she said.

The group came to a small clearing. In the center stood a broken-down cabin. Its windows were cracked, and the door hung open, creaking in the wind.

Jake felt his heart pound. “This must be the place.”

Inside, the cabin smelled of mold and old wood. Broken chairs lay scattered, and spider webs filled the corners.

Elena’s scanner beeped loudly. She followed the signal to a trapdoor hidden under a tattered rug.

With some effort, Derek and Jake lifted it open. A dark staircase led down.

“We need to see what’s down there,” Elena said.

The air was cold and damp. The stairs creaked as they descended into a small basement. The walls were covered in strange symbols glowing faintly in the dark.

In the center of the room was a dusty wooden box.

Jake slowly opened it. Inside were old tools, a faded map of the forest, and a small silver dagger.

Elena picked up the dagger. “This looks special.”

Ben’s words came back to Jake — about the Guardians and their fight against the Shadow Man.

“We have to be ready,” Jake said.

Suddenly, the door slammed shut behind them.

The temperature dropped.

A low growl echoed.

“Run!” Derek shouted.

They sprinted up the stairs as something unseen chased them.

Bursting out of the cabin, they didn’t stop until the trees gave way to the town lights.

Breathing hard, Jake looked back. For a moment, he thought he saw a dark shape watching from the shadows.

They were not alone.
