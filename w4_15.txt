

  After the light faded in the underground chamber, Jake, Elena, and Derek thought the Shadow Man had disappeared. But the forest outside was still full of danger.

They hurried back through the tunnel, their footsteps echoing in the cold stone walls.

When they finally climbed out, night had fallen. The moon was hidden behind dark clouds, and the wind whispered through the trees.

Suddenly, Jake stopped.

“I feel like we’re being watched,” he said.

Elena scanned the area with her device, but it showed nothing.

Derek tightened his grip on his bat.

Then, from the shadows, a chilling voice whispered their names.

“You cannot escape.”

A cold hand grabbed Jake’s shoulder.

He spun around.

The Shadow Man was closer than ever.

Its dark shape moved fast.

Jake pushed Derek and Elena forward and faced the creature alone.

The Shadow Man reached for Jake’s throat.

Jake raised the silver dagger.

The blade glowed faintly.

The Shadow Man hissed and pulled back.

Jake ran after his friends as the creature vanished into the forest.

Breathing hard, Jake said, “That was too close.”

Elena nodded. “It’s stronger than before.”

Derek looked determined. “We have to keep fighting.”
