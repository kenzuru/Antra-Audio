

  Robert’s rich dad told him that most people work for money all their lives but never learn how money works. He said that if you want to be rich, you should focus on learning new skills, not only earning a salary. For example, you can learn sales to understand how to sell products, or you can learn marketing to promote ideas. You might also learn management to lead people or accounting to understand numbers.

Robert saw that poor dad valued a stable job and a good paycheck, but rich dad believed learning many skills was more important in the long run. Even if a job pays less, it can still be valuable if it teaches you something new. Skills can help you start your own business, make investments, and create wealth.

Rich dad also explained that fear often stops people from changing jobs or trying new things. Many people are afraid of losing their income, so they avoid learning outside their comfort zone. But to grow financially, you must take risks and keep learning.

The main lesson: don’t just work for money—work to learn. The more skills you have, the more opportunities you can create for yourself.
