
  
  The sun was going down. Suddenly, Jorindel looked behind him.
He saw that they were very close to the castle wall, but they did not know it before.

He was scared. He became pale and started to shake.

Jorinda was singing:
“The ring-dove sang from the willow tree,
Well-a-day! Well-a-day!
He mourned for his dear mate,
Well-a-day!”

Suddenly, her singing stopped. Jorindel looked and saw Jorinda had changed into a nightingale bird!

An owl with big, bright eyes flew around them three times and cried:
“Tu whu! Tu whu! Tu whu!”

Jorindel could not move. He was frozen like a stone.

The sun went down, and it became dark. The owl flew into a bush. Then the old fairy came out. She was pale and thin, with big eyes and a long nose and chin.

She grabbed the nightingale (Jorinda) and went away.

Jorindel was still frozen and could not move or cry.

The fairy sang in a hoarse voice:
“Stay till the prisoner is fast,
And her fate is set,
Stay! Oh, stay!
When the magic is on her,
And the spell holds her,
Go away! Go away!”

Suddenly, Jorindel could move again.
