

  Jake felt heavy as he walked into the library that morning. The town’s secrets pressed on his mind like a heavy stone. The words Mr. Harlow had spoken about the binding spell, the dagger, and the blood stayed with him.

He needed answers.

The librarian led him to a small archive room hidden at the back. It smelled of old paper and dust. Shelves were stacked with yellowed newspapers, brittle books, and forgotten stories.

Jake carefully picked up a large newspaper, its edges torn and faded. The headline read:
“Black Hollow Children Vanish — 1955”

He read slowly:

"In the cold fall of 1955, five children disappeared near the forest’s edge. Their families searched for weeks, but no trace was found. Some locals whispered about a dark figure lurking in the woods, but police called it superstition."

Jake’s eyes scanned the article. Next to it was a blurry photo of the forest, tall and thick, hiding secrets.

He turned the page and found another headline dated ten years later:
“More Children Missing, Town in Fear”

The article spoke about more kids disappearing every ten years like clockwork. Witnesses described a tall shadow moving through the trees, but no one ever saw a face.

Jake’s breath caught when he found a small photo buried inside the article—a boy with dark hair and sad eyes. The boy looked exactly like Lucas.

His hands trembled. The pattern was clear. The Shadow Man was not just a story — it was real, and it had been active for decades.

Jake took pictures of every article with his phone.

When he met Elena and Derek later that day, he spread the photos on the table in Elena’s room.

Elena’s eyes widened. “This matches the notebook and the symbols.”

Derek frowned. “It’s like the Shadow Man has hunted this town for seventy years. If the Guardians tried to stop him before, maybe their magic can help us.”

Jake nodded but felt the weight of history pressing on him. He looked out the window, thinking about Lucas and the other children who never came home.

That night, Jake’s phone buzzed.

A new message appeared from an unknown number:

“Stop or lose everything.”

His heart pounded.

He felt eyes watching him from the shadows outside.

The Shadow Man was closer than ever.
