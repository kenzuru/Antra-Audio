
       It happened that the King's son organised a ball and invited all the important and wealthy people in the land. The two stepsisters were also invited, as they were considered important among the rich people. They were very excited about the invitation and very busy choosing gowns, skirts, and hair accessories that would suit them. This gave Cinderella even more work, since she was the one who ironed her sisters' clothes and folded their ruffles. The sisters talked all day long about nothing but what they would wear.

"For my part," said the eldest, "I will wear my red velvet dress with fancy lace."
"And I," said the younger one, "shall wear my usual skirt; but to make up for that, I will put on my gold-patterned cloak and my diamond brooch, which is truly one of a kind."
They called the best hairdresser they could find to style their hair and fix their decorative hairpins, and they bought their rouge and beauty patches from Mademoiselle de la Poche.
Cinderella was also called to help them with all of this, since she had wonderful taste and gave the best advice. She even offered to style their hair, which they were very happy to let her do. While she was doing this, they said to her:
"Cinderella, would you not love to go to the ball?"
"Oh!" said she, "you are only making fun of me; that is not the kind of place for someone like me."
"You are right," they replied. "People would laugh to see a Cinderwench at a ball."
Anyone else would have done their hair badly on purpose, but Cinderella was very kind and styled it perfectly. The sisters barely ate for almost two days, they were so excited. They broke many laces trying to pull their waists in as tight as possible, and they were always looking in the mirror. At last the happy day arrived. They left for the palace, and Cinderella watched them go for as long as she could. When she could no longer see them, she began to cry.
