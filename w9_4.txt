
   
 On the third night, the boy lay in bed, but he did not sleep. He kept his sword close.

At midnight, strange sounds came from under the bed. The boy didn’t move. He listened. He heard whispering, giggling, and the sound of something rolling.

Then he saw it—a golden ball was being passed from hand to hand under the bed.

One ghost reached out a leg from one side. The boy quickly cut it off. A hand came out from the other side. He cut that off too.

More ghosts tried to scare him, but the boy wasn’t afraid. He hurt them again and again. They screamed and ran, forgetting the golden ball.

When all was quiet, the boy reached under the bed and pulled out the ball. It was warm in his hand. He smiled and put it in his pocket.

He had done it.
