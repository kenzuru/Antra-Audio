Tom climbs the ladder to the treehouse. Inside, he sees a dusty wooden chest. He tries to open it, but it is locked.
Looking around, Tom finds a small key under another box. He tries the key on the chest. Click! The chest opens.

Inside are shiny stones and a small golden cup. On the cup is written: “For the Brave.”
Tom feels proud. He whispers, “I am brave. I found the treasure.”
