

  The town square was alive with music, chatter, and the smell of baked goods. Colorful stalls filled the streets. Maya stood near the small stage, her printed story shaking in her hand.

Lina gave her a gentle nudge. “You’re going to be amazing.”

Maya had signed up for the Reading Night after all. She hadn’t told Elias. Part of her wanted him to be surprised—if he ever came back.

The host called her name. Her heart pounded as she stepped onto the wooden stage, facing a sea of smiling faces… and one that made her freeze.

Elias.

He was standing in the back, half in shadow, arms crossed, eyes on her.

She tried not to panic.

Maya cleared her throat and began to read “The Man Upstairs”. Her voice trembled at first but grew stronger. The story was simple—a girl, a boy, a shared roof, and feelings that grew in silence.

Every word came from her heart.

When she finished, the audience clapped. She looked up, but Elias had already stepped away.

Later, as she walked between stalls, Nico found her.

“That was beautiful,” he said. “Was that about someone?”

Maya smiled sadly. “Yes.”

“You okay?”

She nodded. “I think so.”

But her chest felt heavy. She turned a corner—and stopped.

Elias stood there, holding two hot chocolates. “You like cinnamon, right?”

She blinked. “You’re back?”

“I couldn’t stay away,” he said. “I saw your name on the event list.”

“You didn’t even say hi.”

“I didn’t want to distract you.”

She took the drink and looked at him. “Why now, Elias?”

He sighed. “My mom’s okay. Still cold, still distant. But I made peace with it. I realized… being near you is what actually helps me heal.”

Maya looked down. “So you’re staying?”

“If you’ll have me.”

She smiled softly, but there was still something between them. Something unsaid.
