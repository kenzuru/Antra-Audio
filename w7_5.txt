
  
 After school, Lily walks home alone.
She wears the magic hat and feels proud.
“I helped Mia. I finished my test. This hat is so great!” she says.

Then she sees a little boy on the street.
He is crying near a tree.

Lily walks to him.
“Are you okay?” she asks.

“I lost my toy car,” the boy says.
“It is red and small. I can’t find it.”

Lily wants to help.
She puts the hat on her head.

“Find the red toy car,” she whispers.

Suddenly, the ground shakes a little.
Wind blows. The leaves move fast.

A red toy car flies out of the bushes and hits Lily’s foot.
“Ouch!” she says.

The boy claps.
“You found it! Thank you!”

Lily gives him the toy.
But she feels a bit scared.

“That was too strong,” she says.
“The hat didn’t listen well.”

She goes home quickly.
In her room, she puts the hat on her desk.

“You are very powerful,” she says to it.
“But you must be gentle.”

The hat sits quietly.
It does nothing.

Lily writes in her notebook:
Magic Rule #1: Be careful what you wish for.
