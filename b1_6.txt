

  Feeling entitled means thinking we deserve good things without working hard or facing problems. When someone feels entitled, they expect success, respect, or happiness just because they want it, without effort. This feeling can cause a lot of frustration, anger, and sadness when life does not meet those expectations.

The book warns that nobody owes us anything in life. Success, happiness, and respect come from effort, patience, and sometimes facing many problems. Life is not always fair, and bad things happen to everyone. When we expect things to be easy or to happen automatically, we set ourselves up for disappointment.

For example, if you think you should get a promotion at work without doing your best, you may feel angry or upset if it doesn’t happen. But if you work hard, accept challenges, and stay patient, you have a better chance of success and feel more proud of yourself.

Stopping the feeling of entitlement helps us grow as people. It helps us be more thankful for what we have and patient with difficulties. This attitude improves our relationships too, because when we don’t expect special treatment, we treat others more kindly.
