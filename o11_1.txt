
       Once upon a time there was a sweet little girl who was loved by everyone who saw her, but most of all by her grandmother, and there was nothing that she would not have given to the child. Once she gave her a small cap made of red velvet, which looked so good on her that she never wanted to wear anything else; so everyone always called her 'Little Red-Cap.'

     One day her mother said to her: 'Come, Little Red-Cap, here is a piece of cake and a bottle of wine; take them to your grandmother, she is sick and weak, and they will help her feel better. Leave before it gets hot, and while you are walking, go slowly and carefully and do not leave the path, or you may fall and break the bottle, and then your grandmother will get nothing; and when you go into her room, don't forget to say, "Good morning", and don't look into every corner before you do it.'

     'I will be very careful,' said Little Red-Cap to her mother, and shook her hand to promise.
