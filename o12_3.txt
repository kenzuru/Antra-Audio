
    
    When the dwarfs came home, they found Snow White on the floor. She was not moving, and they could not wake her up. They cried for three days. They could not bury her because she looked so beautiful, just like she was sleeping. Her cheeks were still red.

So, they made a glass coffin so they could always see her, and they put it on the mountain. One day, a King's son came to the forest. He saw the beautiful girl in the glass coffin. He told the dwarfs, "I love her. Please, let me take the coffin. I will take care of her." The dwarfs felt sorry for him and gave him the coffin.

The Prince's servants carried the coffin. They tripped on a tree root, and the coffin shook hard. The small piece of poisonous apple came out of Snow White's throat. She opened her eyes! "Where am I?" she asked. The Prince was very happy. He said, "You are safe! I love you; please be my wife."

Snow White was happy and said yes. Their wedding was big and beautiful. The bad Queen was invited. She asked her mirror who was the most beautiful. The mirror said: "The young Queen is much more beautiful."

The bad Queen went to the wedding. When she saw Snow White, she was full of hate and fear. They made her put on hot iron shoes, and she had to dance in them until she fell down dead. Snow White and the Prince lived happily ever after.
