A science company in the United States announced it created three animals similar to dire wolves. Dire wolves lived thousands of years ago and are now extinct.

To do this, scientists started with DNA from modern grey wolves. They added small parts taken from ancient dire wolf DNA. After this change, they produced wolf babies in a lab. These young wolves are now only a few months old. The company says this work is a step toward bringing extinct animals back to life.

Many scientists do not agree with this claim. They say these animals are still grey wolves with only small genetic changes. A real animal is more than DNA. It also needs the right bacteria inside its body and natural behavior learned from its parents and environment. These parts do not exist in the new wolves. For this reason, experts say calling them dire wolves is not correct.

The company wants to gain public attention for its work. It also hopes the project will bring more profit. At the same time, many people argue that money and effort should go to protecting animals that are alive today. They believe saving nature now is more important than trying to recreate animals from the past.
