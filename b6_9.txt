

  Robert says that many people focus only on getting a higher salary. His rich dad taught him that this thinking can trap people in jobs they don’t enjoy. Instead, he should focus on learning different skills, even if the job doesn’t pay much at first.

For example, Robert worked in sales, marketing, and even as a teacher, not just for money but to gain new abilities. He says that life rewards people who keep learning and growing. By learning skills like communication, leadership, and problem-solving, a person becomes more valuable.

Rich dad also explained that being afraid to try new things keeps people poor. If you only work for a paycheck, you miss chances to improve yourself. Sometimes, a job that pays less can give you experiences that later bring much bigger rewards.

Poor dad believed in staying safe with one job and one skill. Rich dad believed in taking risks and building a variety of skills. Robert says that if you want to be rich, you must learn how to sell, how to invest, and how to manage money.

In short, don’t just chase money—chase learning. Every new skill you learn is like adding a tool to your toolbox. The more tools you have, the more opportunities you can take.
