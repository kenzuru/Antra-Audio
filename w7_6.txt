
  
 That night, Lily can’t sleep.
She looks at the magic hat on her desk.
It looks quiet and calm.
But her mind is full of questions.

She gets out of bed and puts the hat on her head.
“Can you hear me?” she whispers.
Nothing happens.

She tries again.
“Hat… can you talk?”

Suddenly, a soft voice answers.
“Yes.”

Lily jumps.
“Ah! Who said that?”

“I did,” says the hat.
“I am a magic hat.”

Lily’s eyes are wide.
“You can really talk!”

“Yes,” the hat says.
“I listen. I help. But I need care.”

“Why did the toy car fly today?” Lily asks.

“You spoke too fast,” the hat replies.
“You must be calm and clear.”

Lily nods slowly.
“Okay. I will be careful.”

The hat is quiet for a moment, then says,
“You have a kind heart. That is why I work for you.”

Lily smiles.
“Thank you. You are special.”

The hat says,
“You can help others. But do not use me for fun or lies.”

“I understand,” Lily says.
“I promise to use you for good.”

The hat glows a little.
Lily takes it off and puts it gently on her bed.

She lies down and closes her eyes.

For the first time, the hat feels like a friend.
