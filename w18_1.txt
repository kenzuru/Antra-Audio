Father Wolf was about to go out hunting when he heard a strange sound outside the cave. It wasn’t an animal he recognized. It sounded small and weak, like someone trying to call out but not very loud.

He went to look. In the grass below the cave, he found a human baby sitting on the ground. The child looked up at him and didn’t cry or run. He just stared at the wolf like he wasn’t scared at all.

Mother Wolf came down to see what was happening. When she saw the baby, she immediately wanted to take him inside. Father Wolf hesitated, but the child was clearly alone, so he carried him gently back to the cave.

Inside, the wolf pups sniffed the new arrival. The human baby crawled toward them and started grabbing their fur. The pups didn’t mind and began playing with him as if he were another member of the litter.

Mother Wolf said, “He belongs with us now.”
Father Wolf wasn’t sure, but he didn’t argue.

Before they could talk more about it, they heard heavy footsteps outside. Shere Khan, the tiger, appeared at the entrance of the cave. His voice was low and angry. He said he had been tracking the human child and demanded that the wolves hand him over.

Father Wolf stood in front of the cave entrance. Mother Wolf stayed behind him, blocking the pups and the human child. She told Shere Khan that the baby had entered their den, so he was under their protection now.

Shere Khan growled but didn’t attack. He warned them that the jungle would not accept a human living among animals. Then he turned and left.

Once he was gone, the cave felt calm again. The human baby crawled back toward the wolf pups and sat between them like he belonged there. Mother Wolf said she would name him Mowgli.

Father Wolf sighed, knowing this decision would affect all of them, but he accepted it. They would raise the human child as part of their family.

And that was the night Mowgli became a wolf.
