One day a fox said to a crab, "Can you run? Have you ever run in your life?"

"Yes," said the crab, "I run from the mud to the grass and back to the river very often."

"Oh, that is nothing," said the fox. "That is not far at all. You have so many feet and legs. I have only four legs. But I run much faster than you. You are a very slow and silly creature. I run ten times farther than you. I never saw an animal with so many legs run so slowly."

The crab said, "Do you want to race me? I know I am small and slow. But let us first go to the scales and see how heavy you are. You are ten times bigger than me, so you must run ten times faster than me.

"Also, you run fast because you have a nice long tail and you hold it up high. If I push your tail down, I think you will run more slowly."

"Fine," said the fox, laughing at the crab. "Do what you want. The race will be very easy for me. Your many legs and your slow head are no good together. I am the cleverest animal in the forest. Even people say, 'Qui-kwat-wui-lai' which means sly as a fox. Do what you like, slow one."

"Let me tie your tail down," said the crab. "Then I am sure I can win the race."

"No, you cannot win," said the fox. "But fine, do it. It will not help you. How do you want my tail?"

Said the crab, "Let me put something on your tail to hold it down. Then I think you cannot run faster than me."

"Do what you like," said the fox.

"Come closer," said the crab. "I will fix it to your tail. When I say 'Ready,' you can start running."

So the crab went behind the fox and grabbed his tail hard with his claws and said, "Ready!" The fox ran and ran until he was very tired. When he stopped, the crab was right next to him.

"Where are you now?" said the crab. "You said you would run ten times faster than me. But you are not even in front of me. You talked a lot, but it did not help you."

The fox was breathing hard. He felt very ashamed. He walked away and never wanted to see the crab again.
