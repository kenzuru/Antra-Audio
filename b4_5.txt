 

  Now that Haw has found the new cheese, he thinks about everything he learned.

He realizes something very important:
The cheese was never just cheese. It was a symbol for what we want in life — a job, money, love, safety, success.

He also learns:

Change happens all the time.

We can’t control everything.

But we can control how we react.

Haw remembers how scared he was at the beginning. But now he is brave. He is ready for future changes. He says to himself:

“I will keep checking the cheese and keep moving.”

Then one day, he hears a sound. Someone is walking in the maze. Is it Hem?

The story doesn’t tell us. But it gives us a final message:

You can wait and suffer, or you can change and grow.

The book ends with a group of friends who listened to this story. They talk about their own lives. Some feel stuck. Some are ready to change.

The message is simple but powerful:

Don’t be afraid to look for new cheese.
