

  Many people have good ideas but never act on them because of fear. Rich dad told Robert that fear is the biggest reason people stay poor. They are afraid of losing money, afraid of failure, and afraid of what others might think.

Rich dad taught Robert that failure is not the opposite of success—it is part of it. Every successful person has failed many times. The difference is that they keep trying.

One of rich dad’s main lessons was to start small but start now. Waiting for the “perfect time” is a mistake. Opportunities come and go, and those who take action move forward. Even if you make mistakes, you will learn and improve.

Rich dad also explained that courage grows when you use it. The more you face your fears, the less power they have over you. He told Robert stories of people who missed great chances because they were scared to take risks.

Poor dad often advised caution and safety, but rich dad believed that controlled risk was necessary for growth. Robert learned to prepare, study, and then take action—because knowledge without action changes nothing.

Rich dad’s final advice in this part: “Don’t let fear stop you. Start moving, and the path will become clear.”
