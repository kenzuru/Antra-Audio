After playing, Lucy sits at the big table. There are many delicious foods: sandwiches, fruit, small cakes, and juice.

“Do you want some juice, Lucy?” asks her uncle.
“Yes, please!” Lucy says. She drinks the juice and eats a sandwich.

Then it is cake time. Lucy sees a big chocolate cake with colorful candles.
“Make a wish, Lucy!” says her grandmother. Lucy closes her eyes. She thinks about happy moments with her family. She blows out the candles.

After the cake, the children play a game. They hide behind the trees and try to find each other. Lucy counts carefully: “One… two… three… ready or not, here I come!” She finds Emma hiding behind a bush.

Lucy learns that family parties are full of fun, laughter, and sharing.
