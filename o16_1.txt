
    One warm day in the forest, a fast Hare and a slow Tortoise were walking along the path. The Hare loved to show how quick he was. He could run very fast and jump easily. The Tortoise was different. He moved slowly, step by step, and he never hurried.

The Hare looked at the Tortoise and laughed loudly.
“Why do you move so slowly?” he asked. “Do you ever get anywhere? You are so slow that it must take you all day to go a short distance.”

The Tortoise did not become angry. He spoke calmly and clearly.
“Yes, I am slow,” he said, “but I always move forward. I do not stop. I reach my goal in the end.”

The Hare laughed even more.
“You think you can compete with me?” he said. “I am the fastest animal in this forest!”

The Tortoise looked at him and replied, “Let us race. I will race you, and we will see who reaches the finish line first.”

The Hare thought this was very funny. The idea of racing a slow Tortoise made him laugh. But he liked to show off, so he agreed. The Fox, who was wise and fair, said he would act as the judge. He marked the starting line and the finish line. Soon, many animals gathered to watch the race. They wanted to see what would happen.

“Ready… set… go!” called the Fox.

The Hare ran forward quickly. In only a few seconds, he was far ahead. The Tortoise moved slowly and steadily, never stopping. The Hare looked back and could not even see the Tortoise behind him.

“This is too easy,” the Hare said. “The Tortoise will take forever. I have time to rest.”
So the Hare lay down under a tree near the path and decided to take a short nap.

Meanwhile, the Tortoise continued to walk. Step by step, he moved forward. He did not look around. He did not stop. He simply walked and walked. After some time, he reached the tree where the Hare was sleeping. The Hare was sleeping very deeply, so the Tortoise quietly passed him and continued toward the finish line.

Later, the Hare woke up. He stretched and yawned. Then he looked around and saw that the Tortoise was no longer behind him.

“Oh no!” cried the Hare. “He must be ahead!”

The Hare began to run as fast as he could. His feet hit the ground quickly. He ran faster and faster, but it was too late. The Tortoise was already close to the finish line. All the animals cheered as the Tortoise crossed the line first.

“The winner is the Tortoise!” announced the Fox.

The Hare arrived a moment later, breathing hard. He felt embarrassed and sad.

The Tortoise looked at him gently and said,
“Slow and steady wins the race.”
