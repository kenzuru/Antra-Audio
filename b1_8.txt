

  It is very important to protect our time, energy, and feelings. Saying “yes” to everything and everyone can make us tired, stressed, and unhappy. The book teaches that learning to say “no” is a very helpful skill.

Setting boundaries means we decide what is okay and what is not okay for us. It means saying no to things that do not fit our values or goals, or that make us feel bad.

For example, if a friend asks you to do something that makes you uncomfortable or stressed, it is okay to say no. Or if your work asks you to do extra hours but it makes you very tired, you can say no or ask for limits.

When we set boundaries, we respect ourselves more. Others also learn to respect us. Saying no helps us keep our energy for the most important things in our life.

Learning to say no can be hard, especially if we want to please others or avoid conflict. But with practice, it becomes easier. Saying no kindly and clearly helps us avoid stress and feel better every day.
