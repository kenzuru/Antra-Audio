It is Saturday morning. Lucy wakes up early.
She is very excited. Today, she is going to the museum with her mother.
Lucy eats breakfast: bread, butter, and a glass of milk.
“Are you ready, Lucy?” asks her mother.
“Yes! I am ready!” says Lucy. She puts on her pink jacket and blue shoes.

They leave the house and walk to the museum. The museum is big and beautiful. It has tall doors, many windows, and flags on the top.
Lucy looks at the museum. “Wow! It is very big!” she says.
Her mother smiles. “Yes, Lucy. Today we will see many interesting things.”

Lucy holds her mother’s hand. They walk through the main door. A friendly guard greets them. “Welcome! Enjoy your visit,” he says.
Lucy smiles and looks around. She sees a large map of the museum and many signs. “Where should we go first?” she asks.
