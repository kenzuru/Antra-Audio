During playtime, Ruby sees little Pip looking sad.
“Oh no! My green leaf fell under the bench,” says Pip.
Ruby hops over quickly. “Don’t worry, Pip! I can help.”
She carefully pulls the leaf out. “Here you go!”
“Thank you, Ruby! You are a good friend,” Pip says, smiling.
Ruby feels proud. She learns that helping friends is fun.
Then Ruby sees another friend, Bella, who is stuck in a small bush.
Ruby and Pip work together to help Bella free her paws. “Hooray!” cheers Bella.
Ruby thinks, “Today I helped two friends. I am very happy!”
She sits under a tree and eats her small snack. The sunlight warms her fur, and the gentle breeze moves the leaves.
