

  James Clear gives a simple system to build habits, called the Four Laws of Behavior Change. These are easy steps to follow when you want to create a good habit:

1. Make it obvious – Put reminders in clear places. Want to drink more water? Put a bottle on your desk.

2. Make it attractive – Connect the habit with something you like. For example, “After I clean, I listen to my favorite song.”

3. Make it easy – Start small. Just 2 minutes of reading or 1 push-up is enough to begin.

4. Make it satisfying – Reward yourself after doing the habit. This makes you want to do it again.

You can also use these steps to break bad habits by doing the opposite:

1. Make it hidden (not obvious)

2. Make it boring (not attractive)

3. Make it hard (not easy)

Make it unpleasant (not satisfying)

By following these steps, you don’t have to depend only on motivation. You use your environment, time, and rewards to support your actions.

Changing habits is not about being strong all the time. It’s about making smart choices that support your goals.
