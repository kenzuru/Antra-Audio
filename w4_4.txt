

  The next morning, Jake and Elena waited for Lucas at the school entrance. But Lucas never came.

Jake’s stomach felt heavy.

“Have you seen Lucas?” Jake asked Derek, a tall boy with a strong build who trained in MMA.

Derek shook his head. “No. But I heard something weird last night.”

“What?” Jake asked.

Derek looked serious. “Some people said they saw a shadow moving near the woods. And strange sounds — like whispers calling names.”

Jake swallowed hard. “Lucas was there.”

Elena checked her phone. “His messages stopped at midnight.”

Jake knew they had to do something.

At Uncle Ben’s house, they gathered in the living room.

“We can’t wait for the police,” Jake said. “They never find anyone.”

Elena nodded. “We have the notebook Lucas found. It might help.”

Uncle Ben looked worried but didn’t stop them.

They decided to explore the forest together.

The woods were darker than ever, full of twisted branches and strange noises.

Elena used her phone to scan the notebook’s symbols.

Suddenly, Derek stopped. “Look!”

Footprints — big, deep, and leading into a narrow path no one used.

Jake stepped forward. “We follow.”

Hours passed. The sun began to set, and the shadows grew longer.

Then, they found something that made their blood run cold.

A torn piece of Lucas’s jacket caught on a thorny bush.

Jake’s voice shook. “He was here.”

Suddenly, a chilling wind blew.

The Shadow Man’s voice whispered in the wind, “You cannot escape…”

The friends held each other tight, knowing the hunt had just begun.
