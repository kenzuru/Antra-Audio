

  After that terrifying night, Jake found it harder to sleep. Every time he closed his eyes, the same dark figure appeared — the Shadow Man.

His dreams were filled with cold winds and silent whispers calling his name. He would run through endless woods, trying to escape, but no matter how fast he ran, the shadows always caught him.

One night, Jake woke up gasping for air. His heart was pounding, and sweat covered his face.

He sat on the edge of his bed and looked around the dark room. Everything was quiet — too quiet.

The moonlight shone through the window, lighting up the empty forest outside. But Jake felt like something was watching him.

The air was cold. His breath came out in small clouds.

Suddenly, a soft tapping noise came from the window.

Jake froze.

He slowly turned and looked. There, just outside, was a small, pale hand pressing against the glass.

His stomach tightened.

“Lucas?” Jake whispered.

But before he could move, the hand disappeared.

Jake’s heart jumped.

Was it a ghost? Or his mind playing tricks?

He didn’t know. But he knew one thing: the Shadow Man was not just in his dreams. It was reaching out.

The next morning, Jake met Elena and Derek at school.

“I can’t sleep,” Jake said. “I keep seeing the Shadow Man in my dreams.”

Elena nodded. “The curse reaches into your mind. It feeds on fear and doubt.”

Derek looked serious. “We need to stay strong. The more scared we are, the stronger it gets.”

Jake looked at his friends and felt a small spark of hope.

“We need a plan,” he said. “We have the dagger, the notebook, and Mr. Harlow’s spell. But we don’t know how to use them.”

Elena smiled. “I’ve been working on decoding the notebook. I think I’m close.”

Derek cracked his knuckles. “Then let’s finish this.”
