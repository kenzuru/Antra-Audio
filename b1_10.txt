

  We cannot care about everything in life. There are many things happening all the time, but we only have a small amount of energy and time. The book reminds us to choose what is most important to us.

The best problems to work on are those that fit our values and help us grow. Life will always have problems, but choosing the right ones helps us become stronger and happier.

For example, if your value is family, you will spend time with your loved ones, even when life is busy. If your value is learning, you will work hard on your skills, even if it is difficult.

Focusing on what matters helps us live with purpose. It helps us enjoy the journey of life and not waste time on small or unimportant things.

This idea helps us feel peaceful and happy because we spend our time and energy on what really counts.
