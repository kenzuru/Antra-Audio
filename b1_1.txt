

  Every day, many things demand our attention. It could be work, family, friends, news, social media, health issues, or small personal problems. We might feel like we should care about everything, but this is not possible. The book explains that we only have a small amount of “f*cks to give,” which means we only have so much time and energy to care about things. If we try to care about everything, we become tired, stressed, and unhappy.

It is important to decide carefully what to care about. For example, it is good to care about your health, your family, your close friends, or important goals like learning or working hard. But we don’t need to care about every small problem, or what every person thinks about us. When we focus on what really matters to us, life feels calmer and happier.

This idea helps us stop wasting energy on small things and spend it on things that help our life. Choosing what to care about is the first step to feeling happier and more free. When we care about too many things, we become worried and tired.
