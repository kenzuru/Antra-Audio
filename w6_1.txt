
  
 It’s Saturday night, and I’m about to do something insane: confess to my best friend, Emily.

We’ve been close since forever. Childhood friends, lunch buddies, partners-in-crime. I’ve liked her for years. She probably knows—maybe even feels the same—but I’ve always been too afraid to say anything. I don’t want to mess up what we have.

But tonight… no more waiting.

I grab my phone, open Instagram, and go straight to her chat. At least, I think I do.

My thumbs start flying, my brain totally on autopilot. 
“I don’t know how to say this, but I’ve been holding it in for a long time. I like the way you talk, the way you smile, the way you explain things. I always find myself staring at you during class, even when I try not to. I think about you more than I should. I know this might sound crazy, but… I like you. I really do.”

I stare at it. My heart’s pounding.
This is it.

I hit Send.
Immediately after, panic hits me like a truck.

“What did I just do—?”

My stomach flips. I drop the phone onto the bed like it’s on fire and dive face-first into my pillow.

“Oh no oh no oh no—what if she doesn’t feel the same? What if she shows it to someone?! What if she never talks to me again?!”

I stay like that, completely frozen, heart racing.

Then I slowly reach back for my phone, eyes half-closed, not ready to see her reply.

But something feels… off.

I lift the screen.

And there it is.
The top of the screen doesn’t say “Emily.”

It says:
Mr. Wilson 

My math teacher.

I stared at my phone lifelessly. "WHATTTTT"
“Why, universe? WHY HIM?!”

Mr. Wilson is my most serious teacher. The man probably hasn't smiled since 1998. He walks like a robot, speaks like a textbook, and smells like chalk dust and disappointment. And now… he thinks I’m in love with him.

I grab my phone, ready to delete the message. But it's too late.

"Seen 10:34 PM"

NO.

NO.

NOOOOOOO.

My soul leaves my body. I drop the phone again like it bit me. I roll off the bed and start pacing like a madman.

“Okay. Breathe. Maybe he’ll think it was a joke. Or a prank. Or—no, this is me we’re talking about. I’ve never made a joke in his class. He’s gonna think I’m serious. He’s gonna call my parents. I’m gonna get expelled. Or worse—he’ll call Emily.”

I stop.

What if Emily finds out because of THIS?

Suddenly, my phone buzzes.

1 new message from Mr. Wilson.

I almost faint.

My thumb hovers over the screen. I take a deep breath and open the message.
“I appreciate your honesty and passion. It's rare to see a student express such love for mathematics. Keep up the enthusiasm.”

…

Wait. What?

Did—did he think my message was about… math?

I scroll up to read my own words again:

"I like the way you explain things... I keep staring at you in class… I’ve been waiting for the right time to say this…"

Oh. My. God.

Mr. Wilson thinks I’m in love with algebra.

I don’t know whether to laugh or cry.

But right now, I’m just grateful. So grateful.

At least he didn’t think I was crushing on him.

He thinks I’m just weirdly obsessed with math.

Which is still embarrassing—but way better than romantic confession to your middle-aged teacher embarrassing.

I let out the biggest sigh of relief and bury my face in the pillow again.

Disaster mostly avoided.

Kind of.

Maybe.
