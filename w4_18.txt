

 The cave was quiet now. The children huddled close, their eyes wide but safe. Jake, Elena, Derek, and Lucas stood together, breathing hard, still shaken by the battle.

“We did it,” Elena whispered. “We really did it.”

But Jake wasn’t so sure. His eyes scanned the dark tunnel behind them.

The Shadow Man’s scream still echoed in his ears.

Suddenly, a sharp pain stabbed Jake’s chest.

He gasped and fell to his knees.

“What’s wrong?” Derek shouted, grabbing him.

Jake looked down. A dark mark had appeared on his skin — a black symbol, glowing faintly.

“The Shadow Man… he marked me,” Jake said, voice shaking.

Elena pulled out her notebook. “That mark means you’re still connected to him. The curse isn’t over.”

Lucas looked scared. “What happens now?”

Ben’s voice echoed from outside the cave. “You must come back with us.”

They helped Jake to his feet.

Outside, the sun was setting, painting the sky red and gold.

Uncle Ben looked at Jake with worry. “The Shadow Man’s power is strong. You carry a part of him now.”

Jake clenched his fists. “Then we have to find a way to break the curse — for good.”

Elena nodded. “The notebook talks about a final ritual — but it needs a sacrifice.”

Derek’s eyes widened. “What kind of sacrifice?”

Elena looked away. “We don’t know. But it will be hard.”

Jake looked at his friends.

“We’ve come this far. We can’t stop now.”

The forest around Black Hollow seemed to watch them silently.

The battle was far from over.

And the price of victory might be higher than they ever imagined.
