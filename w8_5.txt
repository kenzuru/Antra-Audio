
  
  Jorindel walked closer than 100 steps to the castle. This time, he was not frozen! He could go right up to the door.

He touched the door with the flower, and it opened. He went inside.

He heard many birds singing. He found the fairy sitting in a room with 700 birds in cages.

The fairy was very angry when she saw Jorindel. She shouted, but she could not come near him. The flower protected him.

Jorindel looked at the birds. Many were nightingales. How could he find Jorinda?

He saw the fairy take down one cage and start to leave.

Jorindel ran after her and touched the cage with the flower.

Jorinda was free! She threw her arms around Jorindel’s neck. She was as beautiful as ever.

Then Jorindel touched all the birds with the flower. They all changed back to their true forms.

He took Jorinda home. They got married and lived happily for many years.

Many other young men also found their girls and freed them from the fairy’s cages.
