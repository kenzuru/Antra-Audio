
  
  Jorindel prayed and begged the fairy to give him Jorinda back. But she laughed and said he would never see her again. Then she left.

He was very sad and did not know what to do. He could not go home.

He went to another village and became a shepherd.

Many times he walked close to the castle but saw nothing of Jorinda.

One night he had a dream. In the dream, he found a beautiful purple flower with a big pearl in the middle. He picked the flower and went to the castle. Everything he touched with the flower became free from magic. He found Jorinda there.

When he woke up, he looked for the flower. For eight days, he could not find it.

On the ninth day, early in the morning, he found the purple flower. In the middle was a big drop of water like a pearl.

He picked the flower and started his journey again. He walked day and night until he reached the castle.
