

  Elena sat at her desk, her fingers flying over the keyboard. Her laptop glowed in the dark room, full of code and windows filled with old files. She had been working all night trying to understand the notebook and the strange symbols that had haunted Black Hollow for decades.

“Almost there,” she whispered, biting her lip.

The notebook contained symbols that matched an ancient language she had read about once in a forgotten corner of the internet. It was a language used in old magic — spells to trap and bind dark spirits.

Elena scanned the pages again, comparing the symbols with what she found online.

Suddenly, her screen flashed with a message:

“The binding ritual needs a place of power — a well in the forest where the Shadow Man was first trapped.”

Elena’s eyes widened. She grabbed her phone and texted Jake and Derek:

“Found something important. The well in the forest is the key.”

Jake called her right away.

“Explain!” he demanded.

Elena took a deep breath. “The Guardians didn’t just use the cabin. The real magic was done at a secret well deep in the forest. That’s where the Shadow Man was trapped long ago. If we can find it, we can try the binding spell.”

Derek was silent for a moment. Then he said, “Then that’s where we go.”

Jake nodded. “We don’t have a choice.”

The next day, the three friends prepared for the journey. Elena brought her laptop and a small scanner, Derek carried his bat and a flashlight, and Jake had the rusty key and the notebook.

The forest was colder than before. The wind whispered strange words through the leaves.

They followed old maps and Elena’s digital guides, stepping carefully over roots and rocks.

After hours of walking, they came to a moss-covered stone well hidden by thick branches.

“It looks abandoned,” Jake said.

Elena scanned the area with her device. “There’s still power here.”

Derek touched the cold stones. “We need to be quick.”

Suddenly, a cold shadow passed over them.

Jake turned around.

The Shadow Man was watching.
