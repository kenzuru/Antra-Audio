

  Haw keeps walking through the maze. Some paths are empty. Some are scary. But he keeps going. He finds a little cheese here and there. It’s not much, but it gives him energy.

He laughs more. He feels proud. He is not the same as before. He is stronger now. He starts to enjoy the journey, not just the cheese.

He writes more messages on the wall:

“When you move past your fear, you feel free.”

“The quicker you let go of old cheese, the sooner you find new cheese.”

These lessons are not just about cheese. They are about life. When we lose a job, a friend, or something we love, it hurts. But if we keep moving, we might find something new — maybe even better.

Haw also thinks about Hem. He still hopes Hem will follow the signs and find his way.

Finally, after a long journey, Haw sees something: a big room full of cheese! He is happy. He eats. He rests. He has found new cheese.

But he doesn’t forget the lesson: change will happen again. Be ready.
