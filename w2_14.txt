

  The next day was eventful—and loud.

Lina’s flower shop next door was holding a “Spring Opening” sale. The street was full of people, music, and stands with tea, pastries, and small crafts. Maya set up a small table outside the bookstore with classic romance books and bookmarks she made by hand.

Claire stayed in town another day and offered to help. “I’m good with people,” she said. And it was true—within an hour, she had charmed half the crowd.

Elias kept out of sight, fixing shelves and checking inventory. But Maya noticed he looked tense.

“You okay?” she asked when she brought him lemonade.

“I just feel… crowded. I didn’t expect Claire to stay.”

“You can talk to her,” Maya said. “Or tell her to go. It’s your choice.”

Before he could reply, a loud beep-beep-beep came from the flower shop.

Smoke.

The fire alarm blared, and people started shouting.

Maya ran out and saw Lina waving a towel in front of the smoke detector. “It’s just the candle display! One tipped over!”

Elias came out with a small fire extinguisher. He sprayed foam on the burning candle and flowers nearby. The fire was out quickly, but the smoke was thick, and people coughed as they backed away.

Claire helped carry boxes out of the shop. Maya helped Lina sit down on a bench.

“I’m okay,” Lina said, wiping tears from her eyes. “Just… scared.”

“It’s fine now,” Maya said. “You were brave.”

Later, once everything calmed down, Elias sat with Maya outside the bookstore.

“She always brings drama, doesn’t she?” he said about his sister, half-laughing.

“She’s strong,” Maya said. “Like someone else I know.”

He looked at her. “Thanks for helping.”

“I always will.”

They sat in silence, the sun warming their skin, the scent of foam and smoke still in the air. Then Elias spoke again.

“I think it’s time I go back. Just for a few days. See my parents. Talk to Claire properly.”

Maya’s heart dropped.

“You’re leaving?”

“Not forever,” he said. “But I need to face what I left behind.”

Maya nodded, even though her chest felt tight.

“Will you come back?”

Elias looked at her for a long moment.

“Only if you’re here.”
