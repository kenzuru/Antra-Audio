

  The night was cold and dark as Jake, Elena, Derek, Lucas, and Uncle Ben made their way to the Whispering Stone. The forest was silent, except for the crunch of their footsteps on fallen leaves. The air felt heavy — thick with magic and danger.

Jake touched the black mark on his arm. It burned slightly, a reminder of the Shadow Man’s power inside him.

“This is it,” Uncle Ben said quietly. “The place where the worlds meet.”

They reached a clearing where a giant stone stood. It was old and covered with strange carvings that glowed faintly in the moonlight.

Elena set up her equipment. “This is the place the Guardians used for the ritual,” she said. “We have to finish what they started.”

Jake looked at his friends. “Are you ready?”

They nodded.

Elena began chanting the ancient words from the notebook. The carvings on the stone glowed brighter. The wind picked up, swirling around them.

The Shadow Man appeared, a dark figure rising from the shadows. His voice was a cold whisper. “You cannot defeat me.”

Jake stepped forward, holding the silver dagger.

“This ends tonight,” he said.

The Shadow Man lunged, and the fight began.

Jake swung the dagger, glowing with light. Elena’s chanting grew louder, weaving the magic of the ritual. Derek and Lucas defended the group, fighting shadows that rose from the ground.

The battle was fierce. The Shadow Man screamed, his darkness pushing against their light.

Suddenly, Jake felt the mark on his arm flare. He realized the sacrifice — he must give himself to break the curse.

“I’m ready,” Jake said.

Elena looked shocked. “No, Jake—”

“It’s the only way,” he said.

Jake held the dagger high and plunged it into the Whispering Stone. A bright light exploded, and the Shadow Man’s scream echoed one last time.

Then silence.

The black mark on Jake’s arm faded.

The forest grew still.

The curse was broken.

Jake fell to his knees, exhausted but free.

Elena, Derek, Lucas, and Uncle Ben rushed to him.

“We did it,” Elena whispered.

Jake smiled weakly. “Together.”

The sun rose, shining through the trees, lighting Black Hollow with new hope.

The shadows were gone.

But Jake knew — some secrets never fully disappear.
