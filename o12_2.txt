
    
      Snow White was alone in the big, dark forest. She ran all day until evening. She saw a little cottage and went inside. Everything was small, but clean: seven small plates, seven small cups, and seven small beds. She was hungry, so she ate a little from each plate. She was tired, so she tried the beds. The seventh bed was just right, and she fell asleep.

When it was dark, the seven dwarfs who owned the cottage came home from working in the mountains. They saw Snow White sleeping and were surprised. "What a beautiful child!" they said. In the morning, Snow White woke up and told them her story. The dwarfs said, "You can stay here and keep the house clean. But you must never open the door to anyone! Your stepmother will try to hurt you."

The bad Queen asked her mirror again. The mirror said: "Snow White is still alive with the seven dwarfs. She is the most beautiful."

The Queen was full of hate. She painted her face and dressed like an old woman selling laces. Snow White opened the door and let the woman tie the laces on her dress. The Queen pulled the lace too tight, and Snow White fell down, unable to breathe. But the dwarfs came home, cut the lace, and Snow White woke up.

The Queen tried a second time with a poisonous comb. She dressed like a different old woman. Snow White let her comb her hair and fell down, unable to move. The dwarfs came home, found the comb, pulled it out, and Snow White woke up.

The Queen made a very poisonous apple. She dressed like a farmer's wife and offered the apple. The Queen ate the white side, and Snow White ate the red side. Snow White fell down and did not move. The Queen asked her mirror, and this time, the mirror finally said: "Queen, you are the most beautiful of all."
