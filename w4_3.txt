

  The next day, Jake couldn’t stop thinking about what Lucas said — the Shadow Man, the missing kids, the dark forest. The woods behind Uncle Ben’s house looked more dangerous now.

At school, Jake sat alone. He noticed some kids staring at him but no one talked. Then Elena walked in. She had short black hair and bright green eyes behind thick glasses.

“New here, huh?” she asked quietly.

Jake nodded. “Yeah. Jake.”

“Elena,” she said with a small smile. “If you want, I can show you around.”

Jake was surprised. “Thanks.”

After school, Elena took Jake to a small park near the edge of town.

“People don’t come here much,” she said. “They say the woods are cursed.”

Jake looked at the trees. The wind moved the branches like whispering voices.

“What do you mean cursed?” Jake asked.

Elena looked around and lowered her voice. “Every ten years, kids disappear. No one knows why. The police don’t find anything. It’s like they vanish into thin air.”

Jake shivered. “The Shadow Man?”

Elena nodded. “Most think it’s just a story. But I’ve seen strange things on my computer—reports, old news, missing people all connected to Black Hollow.”

Jake’s phone buzzed. It was a message from Lucas.

“Meet me near the old well after dark. I have something to show you.”

Jake looked at Elena. “I think I have to go.”

Elena frowned. “Be careful.”

At night, Jake walked quietly to the old well in the middle of the woods. The moon was full, but the forest was dark and still.

Lucas was waiting, flashlight in hand.

“I found this,” Lucas said, holding a worn notebook. “It belonged to my brother.”

Jake took it. The pages were yellow and fragile. There were drawings of strange symbols and maps of the forest.

Suddenly, a cold wind blew. Jake felt eyes watching them.

“Did you hear that?” Lucas whispered.

A shadow moved between the trees.

Jake’s heart pounded. “We should get out of here.”

But it was too late.

From the darkness, a tall, black figure stepped forward—no face, only darkness.

“The Shadow Man.”

Jake, Lucas, and Elena ran fast, their breaths loud in the night.

They didn’t stop until the trees ended and the town lights shone ahead.

Jake looked back once.

The Shadow Man was watching.
