

  The writer, Robert, had two dads. One was his real father. He was well-educated and had a good job. Robert calls him the “Poor Dad.” The other was the father of his best friend. He did not go to college, but he became rich. Robert calls him the “Rich Dad.”

Both dads taught him how to live, but their ideas were very different. The Poor Dad said, “Go to school, get good grades, and find a safe job.” The Rich Dad said, “Learn how money works, and make money work for you.”

Robert saw how both men lived. Poor Dad worked hard, but always had money problems. Rich Dad owned businesses and had people work for him. He was free and had more choices in life.

The big lesson here is: School teaches you to work for money, but not how to make money work for you. That is what Rich Dad taught Robert. This was the start of his financial education.

In this part, we learn that having money is not just about working hard. It’s about learning how money moves and grows. If you follow only what schools and parents tell you, you may never learn about wealth.

Robert decided to listen more to his Rich Dad, and this changed his life. He learned that mindset is more important than just a job. That’s the beginning of becoming rich.
