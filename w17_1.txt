Tom is a boy. He loves boats and machines.
One day, he watches a motorboat race on the lake.
He says, “I want to make my own racing boat!”

Tom goes to his garage.
He has wood, a small engine, screws, nails, and tools.
He draws a plan for his boat. It is small, red, and very fast.

Every day, Tom works on the boat.
He cuts the wood, fixes the engine, and paints the boat.
Sometimes, the engine does not work.
Tom feels frustrated. “Oh no! I must check it again,” he says.
He reads a book about engines and asks his dad for help.

Finally, after many days, the boat is ready.
It is small but strong. The engine is fixed.
The boat is red and shiny.
Tom smiles. “Tomorrow, I will try it on the lake!”

That night, Tom dreams about the race.
He imagines the boat moving very fast, water splashing everywhere.
He wakes up early. He cannot wait to see it move.
