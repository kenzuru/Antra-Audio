
  
 Lily is walking home from school.
She is a small girl with short brown hair.
She wears a red jacket and carries a blue bag.

It is a cold day.
The wind is strong.
Leaves fly in the air.
The sky is gray, and clouds move fast.

As Lily walks past the park, she sees something strange.
There is a big, black hat on the ground.
It is round and soft.
It looks old but clean.

“Whose hat is this?” Lily asks.
She looks around.
No one is near.
No man, no woman, no child.

She picks up the hat.
It feels warm in her hands.
She looks inside.
There is no name and no note.

Lily smiles.
“I will take it home,” she says.
She puts the hat in her bag and walks home.

But the hat is not a normal hat.
It is a magic hat.
