

  In this part, Rich Dad teaches Robert an important lesson: The rich don’t work for money — they make money work for them.

When Robert was young, he worked for Rich Dad at one of his stores. The pay was very small. At first, Robert was angry and wanted more money. But Rich Dad explained that this was not about pay — it was about learning.

Rich Dad said that most people work for money all their lives. They get a job, earn money, and spend it. Then they need more money, so they work again. This becomes a loop — a cycle that never ends. Rich people break this loop by building things that bring money even when they are not working — like businesses or investments.

He told Robert to look around and find ways to earn money without a job. This is called passive income. It means the money comes in from things you own — like renting a house or earning from a company.

Robert learned that fear and desire control most people. They fear not having money, so they keep working. They want nice things, so they spend fast. The rich control these feelings and use knowledge to grow their wealth.

The key message is: Don’t just work for money. Learn how to make money work for you. This lesson helped Robert think like the rich and start building wealth early.
