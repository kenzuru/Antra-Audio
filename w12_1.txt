Tom wakes up early on Saturday. The sun is bright. He eats breakfast with his mother.
“Tom, today is Saturday. You can play outside,” she says.
Tom smiles. He wants an adventure. He looks out the window and sees the forest near his house. He decides to go for a walk and takes a small backpack with water and a sandwich.

As he walks through the forest, he sees something shiny on the ground. He picks it up. It is an old map! The map shows the forest, a river, and a big X near a hill.
Tom feels excited. “A treasure?” he whispers. He decides to follow the map.
