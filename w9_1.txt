
  
 Two sisters were walking home from the fair. They were happy, laughing, and carrying small things they had bought. On their way back, they saw something strange—a man standing by a house. He was the most handsome man they had ever seen. He wore a hat with shiny gold on it. He had gold rings on his fingers and a bright red-gold watch chain. Everything about him looked rich and important.

In each of his hands, he held a shiny golden ball.

He smiled at the girls and said kindly, “These balls are very special. Each of you may take one. But listen carefully—you must keep it safe. If you lose it, something very bad will happen. You will be hanged.”

The older sister held her golden ball tightly. But the younger sister was more playful. She loved to toss things up in the air. As they walked near a field, she threw her ball up, caught it, and laughed. She threw it again. Then again.

But the fourth time, the ball went higher than before—and it flew over a fence and out of sight!

She quickly climbed up to look over the fence. She saw the ball rolling over the green grass. It rolled and rolled until it reached the door of a strange old house. Then it disappeared inside.

She climbed down and told her sister. “I lost it… it went into that house.”

Her sister was scared. “You know what he said. If you lose the ball…”

Everyone said the girl must be hanged for losing the ball.
