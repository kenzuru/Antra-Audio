The Little Prince lived in a small castle on a hill. The castle was round and white. It had three towers and a small garden. The castle was quiet and beautiful.

Every morning, the Little Prince woke up early. He looked out of the window. He could see the stars, the sun, and his little rose. The rose grew in a small garden near the castle. The Little Prince loved his rose very much.

He walked in the castle halls. The halls were clean. There were many books on the shelves. He liked to read books about stars, planets, and animals. The castle was full of interesting things, and he explored every room carefully.

The Little Prince also took care of his small volcanoes. He cleaned the volcanoes every day. Sometimes, a little fire came out, and he put it out quickly. “I must take care of everything here,” he said.

In the afternoon, he played in the garden. He watched the birds and rabbits. He watered the plants and talked to his rose. “You are the most beautiful flower on my planet,” he said.

The Little Prince felt happy in his castle. It was quiet, safe, and full of things he loved. He learned to take care of his home, his plants, and his animals.
