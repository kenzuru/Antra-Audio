
  
 The next morning, Lily wakes up early.
She puts on her clothes and the magic hat.
She wants to help more people today.

Lily walks to the park near her house.
She sees many people there.

An old man is sitting on a bench.
He looks tired and sad.
Lily walks to him.

“Hello, sir,” she says politely.
“Are you okay?”

The man smiles a little.
“I lost my glasses,” he says.
“I cannot see well without them.”

Lily thinks for a moment.
She puts on the magic hat.
“Please, find the old man’s glasses,” she whispers softly.

The wind blows.
Leaves fly around.
Suddenly, a pair of glasses falls from a tree branch near the man.

“Here are your glasses!” Lily says, picking them up.

The old man puts on the glasses and smiles.
“Thank you, young lady,” he says.
“You are very kind.”

Lily feels happy inside.
The magic hat helps people.
But she remembers the hat’s words:
“Use magic for good.”

She takes off the hat and smiles.
“Today is a good day.”
