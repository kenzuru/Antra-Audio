

  When the cheese disappears, the four friends react differently.

Sniff and Scurry are not surprised. They saw the signs. They quickly tie their shoes and run into the maze. They want to find new cheese. They don’t waste time being angry.

Hem and Haw are shocked. They keep returning to Station C, hoping the cheese will come back. Hem says, “Who moved my cheese? It’s not fair!” He gets upset and refuses to leave.

Haw is also afraid. He doesn’t want to go into the maze. He worries about getting lost. But inside, he starts to think: “What if there is better cheese out there?”

Days go by. Hem stays angry. Haw begins to laugh at himself. He starts to understand that change happens and maybe it’s not so bad.

This part of the story shows two big ideas:

People react to change in different ways.

If we don’t move, we might miss new chances.

Sometimes, we wait too long, hoping things will return to normal. But the truth is: life keeps moving, and so should we.
