
      
     A man and a woman lived in a house. They were very sad because they had wanted a child for a very long time, but they had none. Behind their house, there was a big garden full of the most beautiful flowers and plants. But a tall stone wall was all around it. A very powerful witch lived there, and everyone was afraid to go near her garden.

One day, the woman looked into the garden from her window. She saw some green leaves called rapunzel. They looked so fresh! She wanted to eat them so much. Her wish was so strong that she became very pale and sick, and she could not eat anything else. Her husband was worried and asked, "What is wrong?" She said, "I will die if I do not eat the rapunzel from that garden."

The husband loved his wife very much. He thought, "I cannot let my wife die." So, at night, he climbed the tall wall. He quickly took a handful of rapunzel leaves and brought them to his wife. She ate them right away and loved the taste. It was so good that she wanted three times more the next day. The man knew he had to go back.

The second night, the witch saw him! She was very angry. "How dare you steal from my garden like a thief?" she shouted. The man cried, "Please, do not hurt me! My wife will die without it." The witch became less angry and said, "I will let you take all the rapunzel you want. But you must promise me one thing: when your baby is born, you must give it to me. I will look after it like a mother." The man was so frightened of the witch that he agreed to everything.

When the baby girl was born, the witch came right away. She named the child Rapunzel and took her away.
