

  In this part, Rich Dad teaches Robert to "mind your own business." This doesn’t mean to ignore others — it means: focus on building your own path to wealth.

Most people work all their lives in someone else’s business. They work for a boss, a company, or the government. Rich Dad says that is not bad — but if you want to be rich, you must also build your own business or asset.

This does not mean starting a big company right away. It can be small. It can be buying one rental house or creating a side business. The idea is to build something that brings money, even when you are not working.

Robert also learned to keep his day job, but start buying assets on the side. Many people spend all their extra money on fun or luxury. But the rich use extra money to build things. Even buying a few stocks or saving to buy property is a good start.

Another lesson in this part is about not depending only on a salary. A job can be lost. Salaries do not always grow. But if you have your own business or investments, they can keep growing. That is how rich people stay rich — they protect and grow their money.

Rich Dad says: You must control your money — not let others control it. Working for others is okay, but never forget to work for your own future too.

The key message is: Build your own assets. Pay yourself first. That’s how you get rich slowly.
