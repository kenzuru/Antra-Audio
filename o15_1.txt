
    
    Once there was a man who married, for his second wife, the proudest and most arrogant woman anyone had ever seen. She had, from a first husband, two daughters who were exactly like her in every way. He also had, from another wife, a young daughter with a very kind and sweet nature, which she got from her mother, who was the best person in the world.

Soon after the wedding, the stepmother began to show her true character. She did not like the good qualities of this pretty girl, especially because they made her own daughters look worse by comparison. She made her do the hardest and dirtiest work in the house: she washed the dishes and tables, and cleaned her own room and the rooms of the stepmother's daughters. She slept in a poor attic room on an old straw bed, while her stepsisters slept in beautiful rooms with lovely wooden floors, in the most fashionable beds, and with mirrors so large they could see themselves from head to toe.

The poor girl accepted all of this quietly and was afraid to tell her father, who would have been angry with her, because his wife had complete control over him. When she had finished her work, she would go to the corner by the fireplace and sit among the cinders and ashes, which is why people often called her Cinderwench. But the younger stepsister, who was a little less mean than the older one, called her Cinderella. Even so, Cinderella, despite her poor and old clothes, was much more beautiful than her stepsisters, even though they were always dressed in the finest clothes.
