

  One of the most powerful ideas in Atomic Habits is this: Your habits show who you believe you are. James Clear says that lasting change comes from a change in identity.

There are three layers of change:

1. Outcome – “I want to lose weight.”

2. Process – “I will eat healthy and exercise.”

3. Identity – “I am a healthy person.”

Most people start with the outcome. But real change starts with identity. When you believe something about yourself, you act like it. For example, if you say, “I’m a reader,” you’ll want to read every day. If you say, “I’m a runner,” you’ll want to run.

So instead of trying to change your results, change how you see yourself. Ask: “What kind of person would do this?” Then act like that person. Every small action becomes a “vote” for your new identity.

If you study English every day, you are becoming an English learner. If you save money every week, you are becoming a smart saver. These actions build your new identity—one habit at a time.
