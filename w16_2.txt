The next day, Emma wakes up early again. She feels excited to see Lily.

Emma helps her mother with the baby. She brings diapers, wipes, and clothes. She learns how to hold Lily carefully. “I will be very gentle,” she says.

When Lily cries, Emma pats her gently on the back. “It’s okay, Lily. I am here. You are safe,” she whispers. Lily calms down slowly.

Emma also plays with Lily. She shows her soft toys and reads small storybooks. Lily listens quietly. Sometimes, she smiles when she hears Emma’s voice.

Emma feels happy. She learns that being a big sister is fun. She also learns that it is a lot of work. She must feed Lily, help her mother, change diapers, and be patient when Lily cries.

During the afternoon, Emma sings a small song for Lily. She dances a little with the baby in her arms. “Do you like the song, Lily?” she asks. Lily kicks her little legs and smiles.

In the evening, Emma sits beside Lily’s cradle. She looks at the small face and soft hands. She whispers, “Good night, Lily. I will always take care of you.”
