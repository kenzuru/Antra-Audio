Emma wakes up early. She goes to the kitchen for breakfast.
Her mother looks very happy. “Emma,” she says, “I have a surprise for you.”

Emma puts down her spoon. “A surprise? What is it?” she asks, excited and curious.

“Yes,” says her mother. “You have a new little sister!”

Emma feels a mix of happiness and nervousness. “A sister? Really?” she asks.

Her mother smiles. “Yes. Her name is Lily. She is very small. She needs your help.”

Emma thinks for a moment. “I will take care of her. I want to help,” she says proudly.

Later, Emma sees Lily for the first time. Lily is tiny and sleeping in a small cradle. Her hair is soft and shiny. Emma touches her little hand gently. “Hello, Lily,” she whispers. “I am your big sister.”

Her father smiles. “Lily is lucky to have you, Emma. You will be a wonderful big sister.”

Emma looks at Lily carefully. She notices the little movements, the soft breathing, and the tiny fingers. “She is so small,” she says. “I must be careful.”

Throughout the morning, Emma helps her mother. She brings a soft blanket and a small toy for Lily. She talks softly to the baby: “Don’t be scared, Lily. I am here.”

Emma feels proud. She understands that having a sister is a big responsibility. But she also feels happy. She has someone to love and protect.
