
  
 It was quite by accident I discovered this amazing invasion of Earth by lifeforms from another planet. So far, I haven’t done anything about it; I can’t think of anything to do. I wrote to the Government, and they sent back a small booklet on how to fix and take care of wooden houses. Anyway, the whole thing is known; I’m not the first to discover it. Maybe it’s even under control.

I was sitting in my easy chair, quietly turning the pages of a paperback book someone had left on the bus, when I found the part that first gave me a clue. For a moment I didn’t react. It took some time for the full meaning to sink in. After I understood, it seemed strange I hadn’t noticed it right away.

The part was clearly about a nonhuman kind of being with amazing qualities, not native to Earth. A kind of being, I want to point out, that usually pretends to be normal human people. Their disguise, however, became clear because of the following notes by the author. It was immediately clear the author knew everything. Knew everything — and was taking it calmly. The line (and I still shiver remembering it) said:

… his eyes slowly moved around the room.

Faint chills came over me. I tried to imagine the eyes. Did they roll like coins? The text said no; they seemed to move through the air, not on the surface. Quite quickly, it seemed. No one in the story was surprised. That’s what gave me a hint. No sign of shock at such a strange thing. Later the matter was explained more.

… his eyes moved from person to person.

There it was in short. The eyes had clearly come off from the rest of him and moved on their own. My heart beat fast and my breath caught in my throat. I had found a chance mention of a completely unknown race. Clearly not from Earth. Yet, to the people in the book, it was normal — which suggested they belonged to the same kind.

And the author? A slow feeling of doubt grew in my mind. The author was taking it too easily. Clearly, he thought this was quite normal. He made no effort to hide this knowledge. The story went on:

… soon his eyes looked straight at Julia.

Julia, being a lady, had at least the manners to feel angry. She is described as blushing and frowning with anger. At this, I sighed with relief. They weren’t all non-Earth beings. The story continued:

… slowly, calmly, his eyes looked over every part of her.

Good grief! But then the girl turned and walked away angrily and the matter ended. I lay back in my chair gasping with fear. My wife and family looked at me in surprise.

“What’s wrong, dear?” my wife asked.

I couldn’t tell her. This kind of knowledge was too much for an ordinary person. I had to keep it to myself. “Nothing,” I gasped. I jumped up, grabbed the book, and hurried out of the room.
