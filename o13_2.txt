
     
     Rapunzel grew up to be the most beautiful child. When she was twelve, the witch decided to hide her. The witch took her to a tall tower deep in the forest. The tower had no door and no stairs, only a small window very high up at the top. Rapunzel lived alone in this place.

When the witch wanted to go inside, she stood below the window and called out:

"Rapunzel, Rapunzel,
Let down your hair to me."

Rapunzel had very long, beautiful hair. It was smooth and bright, like gold thread. She would put her hair on a hook near the window and let it fall down many meters. The witch then used this golden braid like a rope to climb up.

After a year, a King's son rode through the forest and heard Rapunzel singing. Her voice was so lovely that he stopped his horse to listen. He tried to find a door, but there was no door. He rode home, but the singing stayed in his heart. He could not forget her voice. Every day, he went back to the forest and listened. One day, he was hiding behind a tree when he saw the witch arrive. He heard her call the magic words very clearly.

The next evening, the Prince stood at the tower. He called:

"Rapunzel, Rapunzel,
Let down your hair to me."

Rapunzel let down her hair, and the Prince climbed up. Rapunzel was very frightened when a strange man appeared, but the Prince started to talk to her in a friendly way. He told her that he loved her singing so much that he had to see her. Rapunzel stopped feeling afraid. When he asked her to marry him, she thought, "He will love me more than the old witch." She said yes. They made a plan: the Prince would bring a piece of silk every time he came. Rapunzel would slowly make a silk ladder so she could climb down. They agreed he would come every evening because the witch came during the day.
