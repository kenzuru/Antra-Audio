It is Tuesday morning. Ruby wakes up early.
She stretches her legs and wiggles her nose. The sun is shining, and the sky is blue.
Ruby hops to the kitchen. Her mother has prepared breakfast: a carrot, some lettuce, and a small apple.
“Good morning, Ruby! Are you ready for school?” asks her mother.
“Yes! I am ready!” says Ruby happily.
She packs her school bag: notebook, pencil, a small snack, and her favorite blue ribbon.
Ruby hops outside. She sees her friend Coco waiting at the garden gate.
“Good morning, Coco!” says Ruby.
“Good morning, Ruby! Let’s go to school together,” Coco replies.
They hop quickly along the path, enjoying the warm morning sun.
