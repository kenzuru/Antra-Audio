
  
 Lily comes home and takes off her shoes.
“Hi, Mom!” she says.
“Hi, Lily,” her mom answers from the kitchen.
“Wash your hands. Dinner is soon.”

Lily goes to her room.
She closes the door and takes the black hat out of her bag.
She looks at it again.
It is still warm.

She puts the hat on her head.
Suddenly—click!
The light in her room turns on by itself.

Lily takes off the hat.
The light turns off.

She puts the hat on again.
The light turns on again.

Lily’s eyes grow wide.
“What?” she says.
She laughs a little.
“This is funny!”

She walks to the door.
She opens it and steps out.
The hallway light is off.

She puts the hat on her head.
The hallway light turns on.

She takes it off.
The light goes off again.

Lily runs back to her room, excited.
“This hat is special,” she says.
“It can turn lights on and off!”

She looks at the hat and smiles.
“I wonder what else it can do.”
