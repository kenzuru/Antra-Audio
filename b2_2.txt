

   This habit teaches us to start everything with a clear goal or purpose in mind. Imagine you are building a house. You don’t just start putting bricks anywhere—you begin with a plan. Life is the same. If we don’t have a plan, we may end up somewhere we don’t want to be.

“Begin with the end in mind” means thinking about your future. What kind of person do you want to be? What kind of life do you want to live? What do you want people to say about you when you are gone? Once you know these answers, you can make better daily choices.

This habit helps you live with meaning. If your goal is to be kind and helpful, then each day you can do things that match that goal. If your goal is to be a good student, you will study, stay focused, and avoid distractions.

Without a clear direction, people often feel lost or unhappy. But with a clear end in mind, you wake up each day knowing your purpose. You don’t just react to life—you live with a plan.
