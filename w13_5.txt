The school day ends. Mrs. Fluffy says, “Goodbye, everyone! See you tomorrow!”
Ruby waves to her friends. “Goodbye! See you tomorrow!” she says.
She hops home with her mother. The sun is setting. The sky is orange and pink.
Ruby feels tired but happy. She thinks about all the things she learned today: animals, colors, numbers, and helping friends.
At home, Ruby eats dinner and drinks water. She hugs her mother and little brother, Max.
“Good night, family,” she says softly.
Ruby closes her eyes and dreams about tomorrow. She imagines new adventures, new games, and more learning with her friends at school.
She smiles in her sleep. Tomorrow will be another exciting day!
