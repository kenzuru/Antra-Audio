

  Haw is tired of waiting. There is no cheese at Station C. Hem still refuses to leave. He is too scared and angry. But Haw begins to feel something different — hope.

He tells Hem, “Let’s go find new cheese!” But Hem says no. So, Haw makes a hard choice. He decides to go alone.

At first, Haw is scared. The maze is dark and big. But as he walks, he feels a little better. He says to himself, “What would I do if I wasn’t afraid?”

Haw begins to learn an important lesson:
Taking a small step is better than doing nothing.

He also writes little messages on the wall, like:

“If you don’t change, you can become extinct.”

“Smell the cheese often so you know when it’s getting old.”

These notes help him stay strong. He hopes maybe Hem will follow him and read them too.

This part of the story teaches us:

Change can be scary, but staying stuck is worse.

When we start to move, we find new courage.

Writing down ideas can help us remember and grow.
