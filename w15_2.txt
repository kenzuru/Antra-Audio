Lucy walks with her mother to the dinosaur room. The room is big and dark, and there are large dinosaur skeletons.
Lucy sees a T-Rex skeleton. It is very tall. “Wow! This dinosaur is huge!” she says.
Her mother says, “Yes, Lucy. Dinosaurs lived a long time ago. They are very big and strong.”

Lucy reads a small sign near the skeleton. She learns that the T-Rex could run fast and eat other animals.
She also sees a triceratops skeleton. It has three big horns on its head.
Lucy touches the glass carefully. “I want to see a real dinosaur,” she says.
Her mother laughs. “We can only see skeletons today, but it is fun to learn about them.”

Lucy takes a photo with her mother in front of the T-Rex skeleton. She feels happy and excited. “I love dinosaurs!” she says.
