

  Jake’s dreams were filled with darkness. Night after night, he saw faceless shadows chasing him through endless forests. Whispers called his name in cold voices that chilled his soul.

One evening, after dinner, Jake decided to walk home alone. The streets of Black Hollow were quiet. Streetlights flickered, casting pools of yellow light on the cracked pavement.

He heard footsteps behind him. Turning quickly, no one was there.

His breath came fast.

As he walked near the old bridge, a cold wind blew, sending leaves swirling.

Jake stopped.

From the corner of his eye, he saw a tall figure standing under a flickering streetlamp.

The figure was completely black, no face, no features — just a shadow.

The Shadow Man.

Jake froze. His legs felt like stone.

The figure slowly raised a hand.

Jake felt a cold grip wrap around his chest. The air grew heavy, like ice squeezing his lungs.

A voice whispered right next to him, barely audible but clear:

“You belong to me.”

Jake’s heart thundered.

He tried to scream but no sound came.

The Shadow Man moved closer. The darkness around it pulsed and spread like black smoke.

Suddenly, the figure vanished as if it were never there.

Jake collapsed to the ground, gasping for air.

His hands shook.

He looked around. No one was near.

But Jake knew the Shadow Man had marked him.

He was no longer safe.

For the first time, fear wasn’t just a story — it was real.

Jake stumbled home, locking the door behind him.

He sat on the floor, heart racing.

The Shadow Man had come for him.

And Jake didn’t know if he could fight what was hunting him.
